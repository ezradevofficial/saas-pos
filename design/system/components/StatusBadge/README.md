# StatusBadge

A short status label (one to three words) beside a document, item or person.

**Tones:** `success` paid, approved, received · `warning` pending, low stock · `danger` overdue, rejected, void · `info` in progress · `neutral` draft · `accent` promotions and loyalty only.

**Props the consumer provides:** `children` (the word) and `tone`.

**Do:** keep the word short and specific; the dot is decoration and the word carries the meaning, so status is never told by colour alone.
**Don't:** invent new tones per module; map every workflow stage to one of these.
