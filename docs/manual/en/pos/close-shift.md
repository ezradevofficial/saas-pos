Close your shift at the end of your work, after you count the cash in the drawer.

1. Tap "Close shift" at the top of the sell screen.
2. Count the cash in the drawer, one currency at a time.
3. Type each amount in "Cash counted in" for its currency.
4. Add a remark in "Note (optional)" if the count looks wrong.
5. Tap "Close shift". To go back and sell, tap "Keep selling" instead.
6. On "Shift closed", read "Expected", "Counted" and "Difference", then tap "Done".

Held sales stay on the till and are not part of the shift you close. The server recounts the expected cash from the records it accepts, so the back office shows the final figures.
