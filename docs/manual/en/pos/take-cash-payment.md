title: Take a cash payment
module: pos
order: 50
permissions: [pos.sale.create]
roles: [cashier, branch_manager]
screens: [pos:PaymentScreen]
---
1. On the sell screen, tap "Charge" with the amount shown. The "Payment" screen opens.
2. Under "Payment method", tap the cash method.
3. Type what the customer gave in "Amount received". The first button, "Exact", fills in the amount due when the customer gives the right money.
4. Tap "Add payment". If you leave the amount empty, the button reads "Add what is due" and adds the whole amount.
5. If the customer gave more than the total, the till shows "Change to give". When the till takes cash in more than one currency, choose the currency under "Give change in".
6. When nothing is "Still due", tap "Complete sale".

To undo a payment line, tap "Remove" beside it. To serve another customer first, tap "Hold sale". To change the items, tap "Back to the sale".
