The overview shows completed sales at the companies, branches and locations you look after.

1. Open "Sales overview" under "Point of sale".
2. Choose the period: "Today", "This week", "This month" or "Choose days", then set "From" and "To".
3. Read "Total sales", "Sales" and "Average sale". Under "By company, branch and location", compare the places.
4. Read "Payments by method" and "Top items".
5. If your companies sell in different currencies, choose a "Reporting currency" to see one total.
6. To find one sale, open "Sales" and type the number in "Search receipt numbers". Narrow the list with "Company", "Branch" and "Location".
