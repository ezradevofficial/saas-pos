Record cash that goes into or out of the drawer outside a sale, such as a float top-up or a payment to a supplier.

1. Tap "Menu", then "Pay cash in or out".
2. Choose "Pay in" or "Pay out".
3. Type the "Amount".
4. Type the "Reason".
5. Tap "Record pay-in" or "Record pay-out".
6. If the till asks for "Manager approval", ask a manager to approve.

The till confirms the amount it recorded.
