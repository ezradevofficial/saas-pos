You open a shift before your first sale. Every sale and cash movement belongs to the shift that is open.

1. Sign in at the till.
2. On "Open a shift", count the cash in the drawer.
3. In each "Opening cash in" field, type the amount for that currency. The till shows one field per drawer currency.
4. Tap "Open shift".

The top of the screen then reads "Shift opened" with the time and your name. If you try to sell without an open shift, the till says "Open a shift before selling."
