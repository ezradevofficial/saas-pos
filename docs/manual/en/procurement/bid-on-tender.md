This topic is for suppliers. Share it with the bidders of a tender. Suppliers do not need a {{app_name}} account: they use a personal link.

1. For an open tender, open the public notice link you were sent. Check the tender, then under "Register to bid" type your "Company name", your "Email" and your "Tax ID", and tap "Send me a link". For an invited tender you receive your personal link by email from the buyer.
2. Open the link in your email. The portal signs you in to that one tender for 8 hours. If the link does not work, it may have expired, been replaced, or the tender may be closed. Ask the buyer for a new link.
3. Read the "Submission deadline", the "Opening" time and the "Weights".
4. Under "Prices per line", type the "Unit price" of each line. Leave a price empty for a line you do not offer.
5. Type your "Technical response" and add your "Attachments": up to 10 files of 10 MB each.
6. Tap "Submit bid". The page shows "Your bid receipt" with a fingerprint. Keep the fingerprint: it proves what you sent.

Your bid is sealed. Nobody can read it, not even you, until the committee opens the bids at the opening time. Until the deadline you can tap "Replace bid" to send a new version, or "Withdraw bid". To leave the portal, tap "Sign out"; open your email link again to come back. After the deadline the page says that bids are no longer accepted, and your link lets you follow the outcome.
