The report shows how each supplier delivered over the last 90 days, unless you choose other dates.

1. Open "Supplier performance" under "Procurement".
2. Set "From" and "To" to change the period. If you have several companies, choose the "Company".
3. Read the tiles: "Suppliers", "Purchase orders", "Average on time" and "Average quality".
4. In the table, compare each supplier’s "On time" and "Quality" rates, "Average lead time" and "Price variance".
5. Tap "Export CSV" to take the table into a spreadsheet.

When nothing was bought in the period, the page says "No purchasing in this period."
