Awarding turns the winning bid into a draft purchase order and informs the other bidders.

1. Open "Tenders" under "Procurement" and open a tender whose bids are opened.
2. Read the "Ranking". Disqualified bids cannot be awarded.
3. On the bid you retain, tap "Award". The dialog is titled "Award to" and the bidder’s name.
4. If this bid is not the top-ranked, "Reason" is required. Type why; it is recorded.
5. Tap "Award tender". A draft purchase order is made from the bid and the other bidders are marked "Not awarded". The order appears under "Purchase order" in the summary.
6. For each bidder, tap "Letter" to download the award or regret letter as a PDF, or "Email letter" to send it. Check the address and confirm.

Open the draft purchase order, check it and submit it for approval. See "Create and send a purchase order".
