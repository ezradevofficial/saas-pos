Three company settings decide how closely invoices must match orders and receipts, and how much more than ordered may be received.

1. Open "Procurement settings" under "Procurement". If you have several companies, choose the "Company".
2. Set "Price tolerance": how far an invoiced unit price may be above the order’s and still match.
3. Set "Quantity tolerance": how far an invoiced quantity may be above what was received and still match.
4. Set "Over-receipt allowance": how much more than ordered a goods received note may take.
5. Tap "Save settings".

Without the right to edit, the page says "You can see these settings but not change them."
