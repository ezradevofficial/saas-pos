Your appearance applies to your account only.

1. Open Settings, then "Appearance".
2. Under "Theme", choose Light, Dark, Executive or Warm.
3. Check "Preview" to see sample controls in the theme.

To go back to the default, tap "Reset to default".

To see how a business's own colours would look, switch on "Preview a tenant brand". Nothing is saved. Tap "Stop the preview" or reload to end it.

At a till, the choice is made on the till under "Appearance" and stays on that device.
