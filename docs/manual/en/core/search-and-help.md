Quick search finds a page, an action, a record or a help topic from anywhere in the app. It shows only what your role allows.

1. Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac), or press / when you are not typing in a field. You can also tap "Open search" in the top bar.
2. Type a word. Results are grouped as "Pages", "Actions" and "Help". After two letters, "Records" also appears, for example a customer, an item, a sale, a purchase order or an employee. Only records you may see are listed.
3. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or tap a result, to open it. Actions such as "Add item" or "Invite user" open the form directly.
4. With nothing typed, search shows "Recent": the places you opened lately.
5. Press Esc to close.

If you see "No matches", try another word or check the spelling.

## The help page

1. Open "Help" in the sidebar, or tap the small "?" beside a page title. The "?" shows the topics written for that page. If none is written, the page says so and shows all topics; tap "Show all topics" to see them all.
2. Type in "Search topics" to narrow the list, and tap a topic to read it.
3. Tap "Download my guide (PDF)" to keep the guide for your role.

You see only the topics for the modules your business uses and the permissions you hold.
