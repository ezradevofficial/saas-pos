Your PIN is how you sign in at the tills. Nobody can read it, not even an administrator. You set it in the back office, with your account password.

1. Sign in to the back office on a computer.
2. Open Settings, then "My POS PIN".
3. Tap "Set PIN". If you already have one, the button is "Change PIN".
4. Type your account password in "Your account password".
5. Type your PIN in "New PIN", then type it again in "Repeat the PIN".
6. Tap "Save PIN".

A PIN has 4 to 6 digits. Avoid repeated digits, runs such as 1234 and common PINs. If your role can approve voids, refunds or price changes, your PIN needs 6 digits.

To stop signing in at tills, tap "Remove PIN". You cannot sign in at a till until you set a new one.
