The dashboard is the first page after you sign in. It greets you and shows widgets such as sales today, approvals waiting for you and shortcuts. Your administrator sets the default for everyone or for a role. You can keep your own copy; nobody else sees it, and it wins over the default.

1. Open "Dashboard" in the sidebar and tap "Customise my dashboard".
2. To add a widget, tap "Add widget". It appears on the grid and opens in the "Widget" panel.
3. Choose its "Type" ("Figure", "Chart", "List", "Shortcuts" or "Approval count") and its "Data". For a chart, choose "Bars" or "Line" and the "Period". For a list, choose the "Rows shown".
4. Give the widget a "Title".
5. Drag a widget to move it, or drag its corner to resize it. With the keyboard, use the arrow keys to move by one cell and Shift with the arrow keys to resize.
6. To remove a widget, select it and tap "Remove widget".
7. Change the "Dashboard title" if you want something other than the greeting.
8. Tap "Save draft", then "Publish" to put your copy live. Nothing changes on your dashboard until you publish.
9. To undo a draft, tap "Discard draft". "History" lists earlier versions, and you can bring one back.

A widget shows only data you are allowed to see. A shortcut shows only pages that are in your menu. If someone saved the draft while you edited, you see "Someone changed this draft"; tap "Reload" and make your change again.
