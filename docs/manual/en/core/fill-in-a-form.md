Forms are the documents your organisation built for itself, such as a petty cash request. Each one has its own entry under "Forms" in the sidebar, named as your administrator named it.

1. Open the form, for example "Petty cash request", under "Forms". The list shows each form's "Number", "Status", "Where", "Amount", "Created by" and "Created".
2. Search with "Search by number". The "Status" filter starts at "All but archived"; choose another status such as "Draft", "Waiting for approval", "Approved" or "Archived".
3. Tap "New form".
4. Under "Where", choose the "Company" and, if it applies, the "Branch" and the "Location". "Whole company" and "Whole branch" leave them open.
5. Under "Details", fill in the fields. If the form has "Lines", add one for each row. If it allows files, tap "Attach a file" under "Attachments".
6. Tap "Save draft" to keep it and come back later, or tap "Send for approval". A form without an approval shows "Send" instead.

The form opens and shows its "Number". While it waits, the "Approval" block says who it is waiting for; tap "Open the approval" to follow it, or "See the full history". See "Decide approvals" for the approver's side.

- Tap "Cancel this form", type the reason in "Why is it cancelled?" and tap "Cancel form" to stop a form that was sent. Tap "Keep it" to leave it as it is.
- Tap "Archive" to take a form out of the list. Choose the "Archived" status and tap "Restore" to bring it back.
