The approvals inbox lists everything waiting for you, across modules. You see only what you are allowed to decide.

1. Open "Approvals" in the sidebar.
2. On the "Waiting for you" tab, tap a request to open it.
3. Read the amount, "Requested by", "Why this route" and "History". Tap "Open the document" to see the document itself.
4. Under "Decide", tap "Approve". If the step asks for a reason, type it in "Reason", then tap "Approve" again.
5. To refuse, tap "Reject", type the "Reason" and tap "Reject".
6. To send it back, tap "Return for changes", choose "Return to", say "What needs to change" and tap "Return for changes".
7. To ask the requester a question first, tap "Request more information", type "Your question" and tap "Send question".

To approve several at once, tick the requests and tap "Approve all". A request that cannot be approved is listed with the reason. To hand a request to someone else, tap "Reassign", choose the "New approver" and tap "Reassign". The "Decided" tab shows what you have already decided.
