When a request waits for you, the email can carry an "Approve" and a "Reject" link. The link opens a short page where you confirm. You do not have to open the app first.

1. Open the link in the email. The page "Approve this request" or "Reject this request" opens.
2. Check the request: who it is "Requested by", the step and the amount.
3. For a rejection, type the "Reason". It is required. For an approval, the "Comment (optional)" may stay empty.
4. Tap "Approve" or "Reject". The page then shows "Request approved" or "Request rejected". Your decision is recorded, and you can close the page.

If the link was used, has expired, or the request is no longer waiting for you, the page says "This link is not valid. Sign in to see your approvals." Tap "Sign in" and decide in the inbox. See "Decide approvals". If the page says "Sign in to decide", sign in and decide in the app.

Keep the email private. Anyone who holds the link can decide until it expires.
