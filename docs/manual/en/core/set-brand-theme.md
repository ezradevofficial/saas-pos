The brand theme sets how {{app_name}} looks for everyone in the business: colours, logos, corners and font. Each person can still choose light or dark for themselves.

1. Open Settings, then "Brand".
2. Under "Theme for", choose the whole business, or one company or branch. A company or branch theme replaces the business theme there once published.
3. Choose a "Preset" as the starting point.
4. Pick the "Primary colour" and the "Decisive action colour". Hover, tint and text colours are worked out for you. Leave a colour empty to follow the preset.
5. Choose the "Sidebar" (as the preset, light or dark), the "Corners" (sharp, standard or soft) and the "Font".
6. Upload "Logo for light backgrounds", "Logo for dark backgrounds" and a "Favicon". Logos are JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB. The favicon is up to 256 KB.
7. Check "Preview". Nothing changes for others until you publish.
8. Fix any failed contrast check. A theme with failing checks cannot be published.
9. Tap "Publish" to make the draft live.

Your changes are saved as a draft as you work ("Draft saved"). Under "History" you can roll back to an earlier version.

Status colours, spacing, type sizes and the focus ring cannot be changed. Printed documents stay black on white.
