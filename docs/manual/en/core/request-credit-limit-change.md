Raising or removing a customer's credit limit is decided through an approval. The new limit applies once the request is approved.

1. Open "Customers" under "Contacts" and open the customer.
2. Next to "Credit limit" tap "Request a change".
3. Choose the "Company" the request is for. The "Currency" is the one of the current limit.
4. Type the "New credit limit" and, in "Reason", tell the approver why the limit should change.
5. Tap "Send for approval". A message gives the request number.

To follow the request, open "Credit limit changes" under "Contacts", or the "Credit limit changes" tab on the customer. Search by number or customer, and choose a status such as "Waiting for approval", "Applied" or "Rejected".

1. Tap a request to read the change, the reason and the approval steps. "With" names who has to decide.
2. Tap "Open the approval" to go to the approval. See "Decide approvals".
3. To stop a request that is still waiting, tap "Cancel request", type the "Reason for cancelling" and tap "Cancel request" again.

If a step shows "Nobody can approve this step", ask an administrator to reassign it.
