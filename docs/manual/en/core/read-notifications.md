Notifications are the messages the app sends you: an approval waiting, a payslip posted, a delivery late. The bell in the top bar shows how many are unread.

1. Tap the bell to read the latest few. Tap "See all" to open the "Notifications" page.
2. Use the tabs "Unread", "All" and "Archived". Type in the search box to find a subject or message.
3. Tap a notification to open the page it is about. It is marked as read.
4. To mark one as read without opening it, tap "Mark as read" on its row.
5. To clear what you have dealt with, tap "Archive". Archived notifications stay on the "Archived" tab.
6. Tap "Mark all as read" to clear the unread count.

You choose which notifications you get, and by which channel, in your notification settings.
