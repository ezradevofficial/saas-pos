"Get ready for your first sale" lists the setup steps. Do them in any order and skip what you do not need today. You can come back from Settings.

1. Open the checklist. It shows "done of total done" progress.
2. Tap "Start" on a step. The steps include company details and tax ID, currencies and exchange rates, branches and outlets, taxes, payment methods, your first items, users and roles, and devices.
3. Finish the step. Tap "Mark as done". To leave it for later, tap "Skip for now".
4. To change a finished step, tap "Review", or "Mark as to do".
5. When you are ready, tap "Go to the dashboard".

## Try it with sample data

To explore the till and reports before adding your own items, tap "Load sample data". It adds sample products, categories and customers. Remove them in one step with "Remove sample data".

## Your first items

Add products one by one with "Add an item", or bring a spreadsheet with "Import items".

Your till works with cash straight away. Add mobile money and e-invoicing before you go live with customers.
