You create the account once for your business. You become its Owner. The trial lasts 14 days and needs no payment details.

1. Open {{app_name}} in your browser and go to "Start your free trial".
2. Choose your "Country" and your "Language", then tap "Continue". The country sets your currency, taxes and e-invoicing, and it cannot be changed later.
3. On "Create your account", type "Your name".
4. Type your "Email address".
5. Type a "New password". It needs at least 8 characters. Avoid common passwords.
6. Type your "Business name".
7. Tap "Create account".
8. On "Enter your code", type the 6-digit code sent to you, then tap "Verify and continue". If it does not arrive, tap "Send a new code".

You then go to onboarding, where you choose your business type and modules.

To sign in later, use "Sign in" with the same email address and password. If you forget the password, tap "Forgot your password?".
