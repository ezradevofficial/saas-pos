After you verify your account, onboarding sets up the till, roles and approval steps that suit your business. You can change everything later.

1. On "What kind of business do you run?", pick the closest business type.
2. Type the "Business" name and choose the "Number of outlets".
3. Tap "Continue to modules".
4. On "Pick the modules you need", switch on the modules you want. The usual ones for your business type are already picked. Each module shows what it covers.
5. Set the number of outlets, POS devices and employees with the minus and plus buttons. Some counts affect the price.
6. Choose the "Billing cycle": monthly or yearly. Yearly gives 2 months free.
7. Read your price on the right. If a bundle covers your modules for less, tap the button that starts with "Use".
8. Tap "Start free trial".

You pay nothing for 14 days. Prices exclude VAT. You can add or remove modules later under Settings, Subscription.
