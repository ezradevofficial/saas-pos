Each module can be switched on or off on its own. You pay only for what is on. Only the Owner can change the plan.

1. Open Settings, then "Subscription". "Your plan" shows your modules, billing cycle, current period and monthly cost.
2. Under "Change plan", choose a "Bundle", or "No bundle, modules one by one".
3. Choose the "Billing": monthly, or annual with 2 months free.
4. Under "Modules", tick the modules you want and set the quantities, such as outlets or devices.
5. If you have a "Coupon code", type it.
6. Read the message under the form:
   - Adding a module takes effect now. You pay the difference for the days left in the period.
   - During the trial, nothing is charged.
   - Removing a module takes effect at the end of the current period.
7. Check "New price" and "To pay now".
8. Tap "Confirm change". If there is an amount to pay now, the button reads "Confirm and pay" with the amount.

A change that waits for the end of the period shows "Changes from" and a date. Tap "Keep the current plan" to cancel it.

During the trial, tap "Subscribe now" on "Your plan" to get your first invoice.
