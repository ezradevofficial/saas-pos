Pay by mobile money or card. If an invoice stays unpaid, the tills stop sending sales and selling pauses. Sales already taken stay on the tills and upload once you pay.

1. Open Settings, then "Subscription".
2. Under "Invoices", find the invoice. Use "Search by number" if the list is long.
3. Tap "Pay" beside it.
4. Under "Pay with", choose a saved mobile money number, another number, or "Card, on the card provider's page".
5. For mobile money, check the "Phone number".
6. Tap "Pay" with the amount.
7. For mobile money, approve the request on the phone. The page updates by itself and shows "Payment received".
8. For a card, finish the payment on the card provider's page.

To download an invoice, tap "PDF".

## Save a payment method

Under "Payment methods", tap "Add mobile money" or "Add card". The default method renews your subscription automatically. Card details stay with the card provider. Tap "Make default" to change the default, or "Remove" to delete one.
