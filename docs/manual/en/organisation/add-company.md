Your organisation is made of companies, which hold branches, which hold locations. One company is enough to start.

1. Open Settings, then "Organisation".
2. Tap "Add company".
3. Type the "Name".
4. Type the "Legal name" as registered. Leave it empty to use the name.
5. Choose the "Country". It sets the currency and time zone. It cannot be changed later.
6. Type the "Tax ID".
7. Tap "Add company" in the window.

To change a company, tap "Edit" beside it. To see archived ones, switch on "Show archived".
