A branch is a shop or office. A location is a place inside it where you sell or keep stock, such as an outlet, warehouse or store. Tills are paired to locations.

## Add a branch

1. Open Settings, then "Organisation".
2. On the company, tap "Add branch".
3. Type the "Name".
4. Type the "Branch code": up to 20 letters, numbers or dashes, such as WSTL. It is used on receipts and reports.
5. Tap "Add branch" in the window.

## Add a location

1. On the branch, tap "Add location".
2. Type the "Name".
3. Choose the "Type": outlet, warehouse, store or office.
4. Type the "Location code": up to 10 capital letters or digits, such as L01. It is printed in receipt numbers.
5. Tap "Add location" in the window.

A branch role covers its locations, and a company role covers its branches. See "Give a person a role".
