Suspend a till for a short time. Unpair it when it is lost, stolen or retired.

## Suspend and resume

1. Open Settings, then "Organisation", then "Devices" on the location.
2. Tap "Suspend" beside the device, then "Suspend device" to confirm.
3. The device stays paired but cannot sign in or sell. Tap "Resume" to let it work again.

## Unpair

1. On the same list, tap "Unpair" beside the device.
2. Tap "Unpair device" to confirm. To change your mind, tap "Keep device".

The device is signed out at once and needs a new pairing code to sell again. Its sales history stays. Unpairing is the only way to cut a lost or stolen till off for good. Sales the till had not sent stay on the device and reach the server only if the same device is paired again.
