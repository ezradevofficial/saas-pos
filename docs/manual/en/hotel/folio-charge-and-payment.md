The folio is the guest's bill: charges, payments and what is left to pay.

1. Open "Folios", search by folio, booking or guest, and open the folio.
2. To add a charge, tap "Add a charge". Choose the "Type of charge", the "Item", "Quantity" and a "Description", then tap "Post charge".
3. To take a payment, tap "Take a payment". Choose how the guest pays, then tap "Take payment". For an M-Pesa request, enter the phone and tap "Send request".
4. Read "Charges", "Paid" and "Balance" at the top.
5. Tap "Print" to print the folio.

Voiding a line, waiving a fee and leaving a balance on account are for a manager. A refund is limited by the limit set on your role.
