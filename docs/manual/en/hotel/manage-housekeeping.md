See every room by floor with its status and cleaning tasks, and give the work to your team. Housekeepers clean from their own list. See "Clean the rooms on your list".

1. Open "Housekeeping" and choose the property.
2. Read "Rooms by status" at the top, then the floors below. A room shows if it arrives or departs today.
3. To add a job, tap "New task", or "Add task" on a room. Choose the "Room", the kind (for example "Check-out clean" or "Deep clean"), who it is assigned to, and a note. Tap "Create task".
4. To give an existing task to someone, tap "Assign" on it and choose the person.
5. Tap "Start" and "Finish" for a task a person is doing. When a room waits under "Waiting for inspection", tap "Mark inspected".
6. To change a room's status, such as out of order, tap "Change status", give the "Reason" and the dates, then tap "Save status".
