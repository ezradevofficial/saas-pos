Book a room for a guest ahead of arrival.

1. Open "Reservations" and tap "New reservation".
2. Choose the property and the guest, then the arrival and departure dates. The departure must be after the arrival.
3. Under "Rooms", choose the room type and the rate plan. The quote updates as you go. Tap "Add another room type" for more.
4. Tick "Charge everything to one master folio" if one bill covers all rooms.
5. Tap "Create reservation".
6. To take a deposit, open the reservation and take it there.

Check "Availability" first if you are unsure a room type is free. Typing a rate instead of the quoted one needs the right to override rates and a reason.
