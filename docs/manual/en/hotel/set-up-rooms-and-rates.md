Describe the rooms of a property and what each night costs. Add the room types first, then the rooms, then the rates.

1. Open "Rooms" and the tab "Room types". Tap "Add a room type". Type the "Code", the "Name", the "Base adults", "Maximum adults", "Maximum children", "Beds" and "Features". Tap "Save room type".
2. Open the tab "Rooms". Tap "Add a room", then type the "Number", the "Floor" and choose the "Room type". Tap "Save room".
3. To take a room out of use, tap "Change status", choose out of order, give the "Reason" and the dates, then tap "Save status".
4. Open "Rate plans". Tap "Add a rate plan". Choose the "Room type", type the "Code" and "Name", choose the "Board" and the "Currency".
5. Type the "Weekday rate per night" and the "Weekend rate per night". Add the "Extra adult per night", the "Child per night" and the number of "Children free of charge" if they apply. Switch "Rates include tax" as needed.
6. To change the price in a busy period, tap "Add a season", name it, give its "First night" and "Last night", and its rates. The season with the higher "Priority" wins.
7. Tap "Save rate plan". Tap "Preview a quote" to test a stay. Nothing is booked.

A rate plan with "Available for booking" off cannot be chosen on a new reservation.
