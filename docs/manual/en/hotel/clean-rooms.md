Your phone lists the rooms you are to clean. You see no guest details.

1. Sign in on the phone. "Rooms to clean" lists your rooms by floor, with the kind of task, such as "Check-out clean", and its status.
2. Tap "Start" when you begin a room.
3. Tap "Finish" when the task is done.
4. Tap "Mark clean" to release the room for the front desk. If you find it unfit, tap "Mark dirty".
5. Tap "Refresh" to get the latest list.

Offline, your changes are saved on the phone and sent when you are back online. If a room changed meanwhile, a message says your change was not applied; check the room and try again.
