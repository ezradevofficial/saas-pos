Every row is one room booked, for the property you choose.

1. Open "Reservations". Search by booking number or guest, and filter by status and dates.
2. Open a reservation to read the stay, the guest, "Night by night" rates and its folio. Tap "Open the folio" to see the bill.
3. To change the stay, tap "Change dates or party", edit the dates, "Adults" or "Children", then tap "Save changes". When the guest is in the house, only the departure, the party and the notes can change.
4. To take money in advance, tap "Take a deposit".
5. To cancel, tap "Cancel stay". The page shows "The charge will be" from the cancellation rule. Give a "Reason" and confirm. A manager can tap "Waive the charge".
6. If the guest never came, tap "Mark as no-show" and confirm.

To make a new booking, see "Create a reservation".
