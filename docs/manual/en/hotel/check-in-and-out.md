The front desk lists today's arrivals, the guests in house and today's departures.

1. Open "Front desk" and choose the "Arrivals" tab.
2. Find the guest and tap "Check in". Choose the "Room" (a clean one), enter "Keys handed over", then tap "Check in".
3. If the guest has no ID on record, an alert says so. Tap "Open the guest" and record it first if your settings require it.
4. For a guest without a booking, tap "Walk-in", fill in the guest, room, rate plan and departure date, then tap "Book and check in".
5. To check out, choose the "Departures" tab and tap "Check out". Read the folio. If "Still owed" shows an amount, tap "Add a payment" and take it.
6. Tap "Check out".

A balance blocks the check-out: take a payment, or ask a manager to leave it on account. To change room, find the guest under "In house" and tap "Move room", then give a reason.
