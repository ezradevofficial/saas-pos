Fees, charges and levies are your own choices. {{app_name}} takes none from a regulator: confirm them against the rules you work under.

1. Open "Hotel settings" and choose the "Company".
2. Under "Times and weekend", set the "Check-in time", "Check-out time", "Night audit time" and the "Weekend days".
3. Under "Early and late fees", choose the "Currency of fees" and type the "Early check-in fee" and "Late check-out fee".
4. Under "Cancellation, no-show and deposit", set "Free cancellation until" a number of days, the "Cancellation charge", the "No-show charge" and the "Deposit suggested at booking". Switch "Require the guest's ID before check-in" if you want it.
5. Under "Billing", choose the "Room revenue item", the "Extra levy tax codes", the "Charge limit per room" and the "Room-charge payment method".
6. Tap "Save settings".
