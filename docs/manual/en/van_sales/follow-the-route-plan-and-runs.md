See which stops are due and how each van's selling day went.

1. Open "Route plan". Choose the "Date" and, if you like, one "Route". Read the stops due, in order, with the state of that day's run.
2. Open "Runs". Filter by date, van, route, rep and status.
3. Open a run to read its "Visits" (arrival, departure and the reason when a visit did not sell), "Sales", "Collections", "Loads" and the "End-of-day count".
4. Under "Sales", a sale marked "On credit" or carrying an alert needs a look.
5. Tap "Open the count" to read the end-of-day count. See "Review counts, credit and unloads".
