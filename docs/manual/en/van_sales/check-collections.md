Read the money reps collected from customers on the road, in cash or mobile money.

1. Open "Collections". Each row shows when it was collected, the customer, the receipt, the method and the amount.
2. Tap "Open the run" to see the day it belongs to.
3. To cancel a wrong entry, tap "Void" on its row, give the "Reason" and tap "Void collection".

A voided collection stays in the records and the customer's credit balance goes back up.
