Check each rep's day after the van returns.

1. Open "Counts". A count with a variance shows "Waiting for a decision". Tap "Open the approvals inbox" to approve or reject it. You cannot decide your own count.
2. Read the stock lines and the cash and mobile money table. A positive variance is a shortage, a negative one a surplus.
3. When the count is approved, open it and, under "Unload", tap "Receive unload". Count what reached the warehouse, then tap "Receive stock".
4. Open "Credit review" for credit sales that went over a limit or were decided offline. Tap "Review", add a "Note" and tap "Mark as reviewed". The balance does not change.

Open "Credit accounts" to see what each customer owes against their limit.
