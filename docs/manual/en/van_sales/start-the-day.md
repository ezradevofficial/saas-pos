Start the day on the van's device before the first stop. You can start without a connection.

1. Open the till menu and choose "Route day".
2. On "Start the day", choose today's "Route", or "No route" to visit customers freely.
3. Tap "Start day".
4. Under "Load sheets", tap "Confirm load" for each sheet marked "Ready to confirm", after you check what is on the van.
5. Read the summary of stops done and sold.

If "No load sheet is confirmed yet", you can still sell; confirm the load when you are online. Confirming needs a connection. A day left open from an earlier date must be counted before a new one starts.
