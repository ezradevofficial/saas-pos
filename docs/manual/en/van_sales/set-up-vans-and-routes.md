Register the vans, then the routes they drive and the customers on each route.

1. Open "Vans" and tap "Add van". Type the "Name" and the "Plate", choose the "Branch", the "Rep" and the "Device". Choose the "Stock location", or tap "Make a new location". Tap "Save van".
2. To change a van, tap "Edit" on its row. To stop it from starting runs, tap "Archive". Its history stays.
3. Open "Routes" and tap "Add route". Type the "Name", choose the "Branch", tick the "Visit days" and choose the "Default van". Tap "Save route".
4. Open the route to see its "Stops". Tap "Add a customer", choose the customer and tap "Add stop".
5. Use the move up and move down buttons to put the stops in visiting order. Tap "Days" on a stop to give it its own days; a stop without its own days follows the route.
6. Tap "Save stops".
