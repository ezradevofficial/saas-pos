Two tabs show how the routes performed and what is on the vans.

1. Open "Reports" under van sales. Choose the period with "From" and "To".
2. On "Summary", read "Visits that sold", "Strike rate", "Sales totals", "Collections", "Cash variances" and "Stock variances". "By route and rep" splits them. "Credit outstanding" shows what customers owe, per currency.
3. On "Van stock", read the quantities on each van. If Inventory is not on, the page says stock levels are not available.
