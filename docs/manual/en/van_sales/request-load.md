Ask the warehouse to load a van before the day starts.

1. Open "Load-out" and tap "Request load".
2. Choose the "Van" and the "Warehouse". Add a "Note" if needed.
3. Under "Lines", choose each item and its quantity. Tap "Add line" for more.
4. Tap "Request load".

The storekeeper releases the load from the same page with "Release load". The rep then confirms it on the van. To stop a request, open it and tap "Cancel load", with a reason. You cannot release a load yourself.
