Choose whether reps record their position at each stop, and how credit decided offline is flagged.

1. Open "Settings" under van sales. Choose the company if asked.
2. Under "Location check-in", choose "Off" or "Optional" for "Check-in". Type "Keep positions for (days)", a whole number from 1 to 90. Only users who may track visits can read positions, and they never appear in the audit log.
3. Under "Credit sales", switch "Flag credit decided offline for review" if you want those sales listed in "Credit review".
4. Tap "Save settings".
