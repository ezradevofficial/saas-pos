A role says what a person can do. Where you give it says where it applies. This is how a cashier at Branch A is refused at Branch B.

## Give a role

1. Open Settings, then "Users".
2. Tap the person's name.
3. Under "Roles", tap "Give a role".
4. Choose the "Role". Use "Search roles" to find it.
5. Choose "Applies to": a company, a branch or a location, and then which one.
6. Tap "Give role".

## Remove a role

1. Open the person.
2. Tap "Remove" beside the role.
3. Confirm with "Remove role". To change your mind, tap "Keep role".

The person loses what the role allows there at once. Their history stays. A person can hold several roles at different places.

The last active Owner cannot be removed or demoted.
