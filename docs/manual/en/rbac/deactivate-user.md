## Sign a person out everywhere

1. Open Settings, then "Users", then the person.
2. Tap "Sign out everywhere".
3. Confirm with "Sign out everywhere". To change your mind, tap "Keep them signed in".

Every session ends now, on computers, phones and tills. The person can sign in again with their password.

## Deactivate a person

1. Open the person.
2. Tap "Deactivate", then "Deactivate" with the person's name to confirm.

They are signed out everywhere and cannot sign in until someone reactivates them. Their roles and history stay.

## Reactivate

1. Open the "Deactivated" tab on "Users".
2. Open the person and tap "Reactivate".

They can sign in again with their password and get the roles they held.
