An invitation lets a person join with a password of their own and the roles you give here.

1. Open Settings, then "Users".
2. Tap "Invite user".
3. Type the "Full name".
4. Type the "Email address". The invitation link is sent here.
5. Choose the "Language".
6. Under "Roles", choose a "Role" and say where it applies in "Applies to": a company, a branch or a location. Then choose which one.
7. To give more roles, tap "Add another role". To drop one, tap "Remove".
8. Tap "Send invitation".

The invitation is valid for 7 days. Follow it on the "Invitations" tab. Tap "Revoke" to withdraw one. The link stops working at once, and you can send a new one later.

## What the person does

1. Opens the link and sees "Join your team".
2. Chooses a password.
3. Taps "Accept and sign in".

A branch role covers that branch's locations. A company role covers its branches. "Whole organisation" covers everything, including companies added later.
