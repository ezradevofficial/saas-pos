A position is a job title in a company, such as Cashier. You choose it when you hire someone or write a contract.

1. Open "Positions" under "People". If there are several companies, choose the company first.
2. Search with "Search by code or title". The list shows the "Code", the "Title", the "Department", the "Grade" and the "Status".
3. Tap "Add position".
4. Type the "Code" and the "Title". Choose a "Department" if the position belongs to one; "No department" leaves it open. Add the "Grade" if you use grades.
5. Tap "Save changes".

To change a position, tap "Edit", then "Save changes". To retire one, tap "Archive"; people who hold it keep it. Tap "Restore" to bring it back.
