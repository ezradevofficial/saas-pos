An employee record does not need a user account. The employee number is given when you save.

1. Open "Employees" under "People", then tap "Hire employee".
2. Choose the "Company".
3. Type the "First name" and the "Last name". Add a "Middle name" and choose the "Gender" if you need them.
4. Choose the "Hire date".
5. Choose the "Branch", the "Location", the "Department", the "Position" and the "Line manager" where they apply.
6. Tap "Hire employee".

The profile opens. Sensitive details, contracts and pay accounts are added there, in the profile’s tabs, by people who may see them. To search for a person later, use "Search by number, name or work email" on "Employees".
