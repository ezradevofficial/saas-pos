A checklist lists the tasks for someone joining or leaving. It starts from a template, when you hire or end the employment of a person.

1. Open "Onboarding and offboarding" under "People". The "Open checklists" tab shows each checklist as a card with its progress, for example "2 of 5 done".
2. To see only one kind, choose "Onboarding" or "Offboarding" in "Kind".
3. Tick a task when it is done. Untick it to reopen it. The due date shows beside each task.
4. To set up the templates, open the "Templates" tab and tap "Add template".
5. Choose the "Kind", enter the "Name", and tap "Add item" for each task. Give each task a title, "Due after (days)" (days after the start or leaving date) and, if you want, a "Responsible role".
6. Tick "Start this template on every hire or termination" to make it the default. There is one default per kind and company.
7. Tap "Save changes". To retire a template, tap "Archive" on its row and confirm. Open checklists keep running; no new checklist starts from it.

People who only hold the view permission can read the checklists but cannot tick tasks or edit templates.
