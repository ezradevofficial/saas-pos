Leave requests are decided in the approvals inbox. Statutory leave types come from the country pack.

1. Open "Leave" under "People". The "Requests" tab lists "Leave requests".
2. Search by number or reason. Tap a request to read the leave type, the dates, the "Days" and the "Reason".
3. To decide a request, tap "Open approvals". See "Decide approvals".
4. To stop a request or give the days back, open it and tap "Cancel leave", type the "Reason for cancelling" and confirm.
5. Open the "Balances" tab to see the days available for each employee and leave type, as of a date.
6. If you may manage leave, tap "Adjust balance" for one person, or "Run accrual" to add the period’s days for everyone.
