A timesheet is one day per employee: hours worked, overtime and exceptions. Approve the days before you close a period.

1. Open "Attendance" under "People". The "Timesheets" tab opens.
2. Filter to the period and the people you look after. Read "Worked", "Overtime", "Late" and the exception badges.
3. Tick the days to approve, or tap "Select all shown".
4. Tap "Approve days". To correct an approved day, select it and tap "Reopen days".
5. To close the period, open the "Periods" tab and tap "Close period" on its row.
6. Read the dialog and confirm with "Close period". Closing freezes the period for payroll. It cannot be reopened; later corrections go into the next period.

Only people who may close periods see "Close period". If offline punches may still arrive, the dialog asks for a "Reason for closing now".
