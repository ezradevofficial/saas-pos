Disciplinary cases are seen only by people who hold the disciplinary permission. Administrators and auditors do not have it unless it is given to them.

1. Open "Disciplinary" under "People". Search by number, or filter by status.
2. Tap "Open case".
3. Choose the employee, then the "Category" (for example "Written warning") and the "Incident date".
4. Write the "Description" and, if a measure is already taken, the "Action taken". Tap "Open case". The case gets a number.
5. Tap a row to read the case. While it is open, tap "Edit" to correct it.
6. When the matter is settled, tap "Close case", write the "Outcome" and tap "Close case" again. A closed case can no longer be changed.

The case is part of the employee's record and is kept after closing.
