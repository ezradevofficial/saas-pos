A shift is a start and an end time. A rotation is a cycle of shifts and rest days. A roster applies a rotation to a group of people and plans their shifts.

1. Open "Rosters" under "People". Start on the "Shifts and rotations" tab.
2. Tap "Add shift". Enter the "Name", when it "Starts" and "Ends" (an end earlier than the start means the next day) and the "Unpaid break (minutes)". Save it.
3. Tap "Add rotation". Enter the "Days in the cycle" (1 to 56) and choose the shift or "Rest day" for each day. A rotation needs at least one shift first.
4. Open the "Rosters" tab and tap "Add". Enter the "Name", choose the "Rotation" and the date in "Cycle starts on". Save, then tap the roster to open it.
5. Under "Members", tap "Add a member" for each person. The "Offset (days)" moves a person into the cycle so people can rotate in turn. Tap "Save members".
6. Tap "Generate shifts". It plans a shift for each member on each working day, up to 92 days. Shifts already planned and approved leave are kept.
7. Open the "Week" tab to read the planned shifts by employee and day. Tap "Add shift" for a one-off shift, or tap a planned shift to "Move shift" or "Cancel shift".
8. Open the "Shift swaps" tab to follow the swaps staff ask for. The colleague answers first ("Accept" or "Decline"); an accepted swap then goes for approval. Tap "Cancel swap" to withdraw one.

Employees ask for a swap on their own pages. See "Use your self-service pages". Public holidays show on the week.
