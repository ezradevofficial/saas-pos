Leave types decide what staff can ask for and how days build up. Statutory types come from the country pack, but {{app_name}} never guesses how many days your company grants: you enter and confirm them.

1. Open "Leave" under "People", then the "Leave types" tab. Choose the company if there are several.
2. Tap "Add statutory types" to bring in the types the country pack defines.
3. A banner "Some leave types need their days" lists types without day counts. On each, tap "Enter days", type the "Days per year" (up to 2 decimals, such as 21 or 1.75), tick "I have checked these days against the law and our policy" and tap "Confirm days". Staff cannot ask for that leave before.
4. To add your own type, tap "Add leave type". Type the "Code" and the "Name", and tick "Paid leave" if it is paid.
5. Choose the "Accrual": "Not tracked", "Yearly on 1 January" or "Monthly". Set the "Days per year" and the "Carry-over cap" (empty means no cap; 0 means nothing is carried).
6. Choose how days are counted: "Working days" or "Calendar days". Tick "Allow a negative balance" or "A supporting document is required" if they apply.
7. Tap "Save changes".

The list shows each type’s "Pay", "Accrual", "Days per year", "Rules" and "Counted on". Tap "Edit" to change a type, or "Archive" to retire it: staff can no longer ask for it, and past requests and balances stay.

After changing accruals, see "Follow leave requests and balances" to run the accrual.
