The "My HR" section is for you, as an employee. It shows only when HR has linked your user to your employee record. If a page says "Your account is not linked to an employee record", ask your HR officer to link it.

## My profile

1. Open "My profile". Read your record.
2. To change your phone, address or emergency contacts, tap "Edit contacts" and "Save changes". Identifiers and pay accounts show only their last 4 characters; ask HR to change them.

## My leave

1. Open "My leave" to read your balances and requests.
2. Tap "Request leave", pick the leave type and the dates, and tap "Send request". Your line manager approves it.
3. To withdraw a request, tap "Cancel leave". Pending leave stops its approval; approved leave gives your days back.

## My roster, attendance and swaps

1. "My roster" shows your planned shifts. A day with nothing planned shows "Not planned".
2. In "My attendance", tap "Clock in" when you start and "Clock out" when you stop. If you see "Web clock-in is not turned on for you", clock on a till or an attendance device. Below, read your days and punches.
3. In "My shift swaps", tap "Ask for a swap", choose "My shift" and a colleague, and tap "Send request". Your colleague answers first, then the swap goes for approval. When a colleague asks you, tap "Accept" or "Decline".

## My appraisals

1. Open "My appraisals". When a cycle is open, write your own rating and comments.
2. Tap "Save draft" to keep them, or "Send to my manager" when ready.
3. When the manager completes the review, tap "Acknowledge" to confirm you have read it.

Payslips are on "My payslips". See "Read your own payslips".
