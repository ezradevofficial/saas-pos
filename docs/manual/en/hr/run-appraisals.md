An appraisal cycle is a review period. Opening a cycle makes one appraisal for each active employee hired before the period ends, reviewed by their line manager.

1. Open "Appraisals" under "People", then the "Cycles" tab.
2. Tap "Add cycle", enter the "Name", "Period start" and "Period end", and tap "Add cycle". The cycle starts as a draft.
3. On the cycle's row, tap "Open cycle" and confirm with "Open cycle". The appraisals appear on the "Appraisals" tab.
4. Each employee writes a self review first. See "Use your self-service pages".
5. To review, open the "Appraisals" tab and tap a row. Filter by status if the list is long.
6. Under "Goals", tap "Add goal" for each goal. Give it a "Weight" (%), a "Rating" from 1 to 5 and a "Comment". The "Overall rating" is the weighted mean of the goal ratings.
7. Under "Manager review", choose the "Manager rating" and write the "Manager comments". People who manage appraisals can also change the "Reviewer".
8. Tap "Save changes" to keep your work, or "Complete review" and confirm. The review is closed and the employee is asked to acknowledge it.
9. When every review is done, go back to the "Cycles" tab and tap "Close cycle". Appraisals of a closed cycle can no longer change.

A reviewer who holds no HR role sees only the appraisals given to them. The employee's own review is shown as written ("Self review") and cannot be edited here.
