Close a deal when the customer decides.

1. Open "Opportunities" and tap the deal.
2. Tap "Mark as won" or "Mark as lost".
3. Choose or type the "Reason". When your company requires a reason for lost deals, you cannot confirm without one.
4. Confirm. The status changes to "Won" or "Lost".

A closed deal can be reopened: tap "Reopen" on it. An accepted quote can also win the deal: see "Record the customer's answer to a quote".
