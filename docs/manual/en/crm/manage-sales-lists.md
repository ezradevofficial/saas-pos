Keep the lists that leads and closed deals draw on.

1. Open "Sales settings" and the "Options" tab. It has three cards: "Lead sources", "Won reasons" and "Lost reasons".
2. To add an entry, tap "Add" in the card and type the name.
3. Reorder with "Move <name> up" and "Move <name> down". Change a name with "Rename <name>".
4. "Archive <name>" removes an entry from the lists people choose from. Tap "Restore" to bring it back.
