A form on your website creates a lead each time someone sends it.

1. Open "Sales settings" and the "Lead forms" tab.
2. Tap "New lead form". Type the name and choose the "Branch" the leads belong to. Optionally choose a "Source" and a "Team".
3. Under "Fields shown", choose what visitors fill in, and under "Fields required" what they must fill in. Only shown fields can be required.
4. Save. A window "Copy this link now" appears. Tap "Copy link", or "Copy code" to copy the code to paste on your website, then "Done". The link is shown only once.

The list shows "Leads received" and "Last lead" for each form. "New link" makes a new link and stops the old one. "Archive <name>" turns a form off.
