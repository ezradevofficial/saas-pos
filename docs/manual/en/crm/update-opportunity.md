Move a deal along its stages and keep its value and date right.

1. Open "Opportunities". The "Board" tab shows the deals in a pipeline by stage, with their value and the value weighted by probability. The "List" tab shows them as a table.
2. To change the stage quickly, use the arrows on a card ("Move <name> to <stage>").
3. To change anything else, tap the opportunity, tap "Edit", make the changes and tap "Save opportunity".
4. To stop following a deal without closing it, tap "Archive" on the opportunity. "Restore" brings it back.

If there is no pipeline yet, the page asks you to add one in Sales settings.
