Teams share leads and have targets.

1. Open "Sales settings" and the "Teams" tab.
2. Tap "New team".
3. Type the name, choose the "Branch" (or leave it for "All branches"), choose the "Leader" and "Choose members".
4. Save. The team is listed with its leader and members.

Tap "Edit <name>" to change a team, and the archive button to retire it. A team can be given to a lead, used in assignment rules and set targets.
