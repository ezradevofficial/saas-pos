Give a lead to the person or team who will follow it up.

1. Open "Leads" and tap the lead.
2. Tap "Assign".
3. Choose the "Owner", the "Team", or both.
4. Tap "Assign".

Leads that arrive without an owner are given out by the assignment rules in Sales settings. See "Set lead assignment rules".
