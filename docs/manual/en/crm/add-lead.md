Record someone who may buy, so you can follow them up.

1. Open "Leads" and tap "New lead".
2. Choose the "Branch". Leave "Owner" as "Me" to keep the lead, or give it to someone at that branch.
3. Type the "Name". Add the "Organisation", "Job title", "Email" and "Phone" if you have them.
4. Choose the "Source" (where the lead came from), the "Estimated value" and its "Currency", and add "Notes".
5. Tap "Create lead". The lead opens with the status "New".

Leads that arrive through a website form appear in the same list. Use "Search leads" to find one, or the filters for status, branch, owner and source.
