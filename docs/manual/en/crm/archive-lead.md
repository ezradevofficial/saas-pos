Archive a lead you no longer follow. Nothing is deleted.

1. Open "Leads" and tap the lead.
2. Tap "Archive". The lead leaves your working list but stays on record.
3. To bring it back, open the archived lead and tap "Restore".

To say a lead will not buy and keep the reason, use "Disqualify" instead. See "Update a lead".
