Give a person, team or branch a target for a period and watch the progress.

1. Open "Targets". Each row shows the target and how much is achieved.
2. Tap "New target".
3. Under "Target for", choose a person, team or branch, then pick which one.
4. Choose the "Measure": "Won opportunities", "Point of sale sales" or "Invoiced".
5. Set "Period starts" and "Period ends", and type the "Target amount". It is in the company's base currency, before tax.
6. Tap "Save target".

To change a target, tap "Edit target for <name>". To stop one, tap "Archive target for <name>". Sales reps can see their own targets but not change them.
