The calendar shows your calls, meetings, emails and tasks by day.

1. Open "Calendar". Choose "Month" or "Week".
2. Use "Previous", "Next" and "Today" to move around. A day with no activity says "Nothing planned".
3. Tap an activity to read it, complete it or change it. Open ones past their time show "Overdue".
4. To add one, tap "New activity", fill in the "Kind", "Subject" and "Starts", and tap "Add activity".

You see the activities you are allowed to see at your branches.
