Keep a lead's details and status current.

1. Open "Leads" and tap the lead.
2. Tap "Edit".
3. Change the details. The "Status" list is on this form: choose "Contacted" or "Qualified" as you progress.
4. Tap "Save lead".

To move several leads quickly, open the "Board" tab on the leads page. It shows "Leads by status". Use the arrows on a card ("Move <name> to <status>") to change its status. You can move only leads you are allowed to change.

If the lead will not buy, tap "Disqualify" on the lead, type a "Reason" and confirm. The reason stays on the lead.
