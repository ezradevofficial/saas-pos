Send an approved quote by email or WhatsApp, or download it.

1. Open "Quotes" and tap the quote.
2. To email it, tap "Send by email". Leave "Email" empty to use the customer's address, choose the "Language", and tap "Send by email". The quote goes as a PDF attachment and is marked as sent.
3. To share it on WhatsApp, tap "Share on WhatsApp". A share link is created and WhatsApp opens in a new tab.
4. To keep a copy or print it, tap "Download PDF".

Under "Share links" you can "Revoke" a link so the customer can no longer open it.
