A pipeline is the list of stages a deal goes through.

1. Open "Sales settings" and the "Pipelines" tab.
2. To add a pipeline, tap "New pipeline", type the name, tick "Use as the default pipeline" if needed, and save.
3. Under "Stages of <name>", tap "Add stage". Type the name and the "Probability" of winning at that stage, from 0 to 100.
4. Reorder stages with "Move <name> up" and "Move <name> down". Change one with "Edit <name>".
5. Tap "Make default" on another pipeline to make it the default. "Rename <name>" renames a pipeline.
6. "Archive <name>" retires a stage or pipeline without deleting it. Archived pipelines can be restored.
