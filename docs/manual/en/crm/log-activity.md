Write down what you did with a lead or opportunity, or what you must do next.

1. Open the lead or opportunity and tap "Log activity".
2. Choose the "Kind": call, meeting, email or task.
3. Type the "Subject" and set when it "Starts". Add when it "Ends" if it has a length.
4. To be reminded, set "Remind me at". You get a notification at that time.
5. Choose the "Owner" if someone else does it, add "Notes", and tap "Add activity".

When it is done, tap "Complete" on the activity, type the "Outcome" and confirm. "Reopen" brings a finished activity back, and "Cancel activity" drops it. Open activities past their time are marked "Overdue".
