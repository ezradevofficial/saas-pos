Track a deal with a customer through your pipeline.

1. Open "Opportunities" and tap "New opportunity".
2. Choose the "Branch" and search for the customer under "Search customers".
3. Type the "Opportunity name", then choose the "Pipeline" and the "Stage".
4. Enter the "Value" and its "Currency". Leave "Probability" empty to use the stage's probability.
5. Set the "Expected close date" and tap "Create opportunity".

You can also create one while converting a lead. See "Convert a lead to a customer".
