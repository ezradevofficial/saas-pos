Rules give new leads that have no owner to the right person or team.

1. Open "Sales settings" and the "Assignment rules" tab.
2. Tap "New rule" and type the name.
3. Choose the "Sources" and "Channels" it applies to, or leave "Any".
4. Under "Give the lead to", choose "Person" or "Team in turn" and pick who.
5. Set the order number: rules are tried from the lowest number, and the first match wins.
6. Leave "Rule is in use" on and save.

Use "Edit <name>" to change a rule and "Archive <name>" to retire it.
