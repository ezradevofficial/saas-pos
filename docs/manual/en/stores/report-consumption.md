The consumption report shows what departments used: issued less returned, over the dates you choose.

1. Open "Consumption" under "Stores".
2. Under "Group by", choose the tab: "Department", "Cost centre", "Item" or "Month".
3. Filter by "Store" and by "From" and "To" dates, or search by item.
4. Read each row: "Issued", "Returned" and "Consumed". If you may see values, "Value consumed" and the totals ("Consumed in" and the currency) appear too.
5. Tap "Export" and choose "Excel (.xlsx)", "CSV" or "PDF".

If nothing was issued for the filters you chose, the page says so. Clear a filter to see more.
