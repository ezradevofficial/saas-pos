The store ledger lists every movement in and out of the stores, with the balance after it.

1. Open "Store ledger" under "Stores".
2. Search by item or document with "Search by item or document".
3. Filter by "Store", by "Movement" (opening stock, goods received, adjustments, issues and returns) and by "From" and "To" dates.
4. Read each row: the date, the movement, the document (tap its number to open it), the quantity, the "Balance after" and the department.
5. Tap "Export" and choose "Excel (.xlsx)", "CSV" or "PDF".

When Inventory is on, it keeps the quantities of the stores. The page then says so and links to "See the full stock ledger in Inventory". The values column appears only if you may see values.
