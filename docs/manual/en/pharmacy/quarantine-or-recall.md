Stop a doubtful batch from being sold.

1. To hold one lot, open it from "Stock by lot", tap "Quarantine", give the "Reason" and confirm. Tap "Release" later to let it sell again.
2. To block a whole batch at every location and till, open "Recalls" and tap "Issue recall".
3. Choose the "Item", type the "Batch number" and the "Reason". Add the "Regulator reference" if you have it.
4. Tap "Issue recall".
5. Open the recall and read "Who bought it" to contact the customers named on the sales.
6. When the batch is cleared, open the recall and tap "Lift recall".
