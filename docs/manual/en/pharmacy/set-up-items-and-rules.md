Say which items are medicines, controlled drugs, poisons or agrochemicals, and set the rules your pharmacy works under. {{app_name}} takes none of these values from a regulator: confirm them against the rules that apply to you.

1. Open "Item profiles". Search by name or code.
2. Tap "Add an item", choose the item, then set its "Class" and "Kind".
3. Switch "Tracked by lot" and "Restricted sale" as needed. Write "Usage notes" shown at the till, the "Schedule or category" in the regulator's words and, for veterinary and agrochemical items, the "Withdrawal period (days)".
4. Tap "Save profile". Tap a row later to change it.
5. Open "Pharmacy settings" and choose the "Company".
6. Under "Expiry", set the "Near-expiry window" and the "Least days to expiry to sell". Switch "Quarantine expired stock each night" if you want it.
7. Under "Prescriptions and pharmacists", choose the "Classes that need a prescription" and the "Classes that need a pharmacist". Type the "Days a prescription stays valid", or leave it empty to skip the age check.
8. Tap "Save settings".
