Keep the prescriptions you dispense against. They hold personal data, so only people who need them can open them.

1. Open "Prescriptions". Search by patient, prescriber, licence or facility.
2. Tap "New prescription".
3. Choose the "Location". Type the "Prescriber", the "Licence number" as the regulator wrote it, the "Facility" and the "Patient".
4. Type the "Patient phone" and the "Patient date of birth" if you have them. Set "Prescribed on" and add a "Note" if needed.
5. Tap "Save prescription".
6. To attach a photo or scan, open the prescription, tap "Add an image", choose an image or PDF and save it. Tap "Open the image" to read it later.
7. To withdraw one, open it and tap "Cancel prescription". It stays on file but can no longer be used.

At the till, attach the prescription to the sale. See "Dispense at the till".
