The register lists every receipt, dispense, return, transfer and write-off of a controlled item at one location, with its running balance.

1. Open "Controlled-drug register".
2. Choose an item and a location. Set "From" and "To" to narrow the dates.
3. Read the entries, or search by batch, patient or prescriber.
4. Tap "Print" to print the register for signature.

An entry is never edited. To fix a mistake, tap "Add correction", enter the "Quantity to add or remove" with a minus sign to remove, and give a reason. Correcting needs the right to adjust the register.
