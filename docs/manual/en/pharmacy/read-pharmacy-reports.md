Three reports show what is about to be lost and what was dispensed on prescription.

1. Open "Expiry report" to see lots with stock that expired or expire soon. Choose "Expires within" a number of days, or keep each company's own window. Write off what has expired. See "Write off expired or damaged stock".
2. Open "Slow movers" to see lots that did not sell where they are held. Choose "No sale for" a number of days. Look at "Days since last sale" to decide what to move or discount.
3. Open "Prescription log" to see the items dispensed against a prescription, with the prescriber, the patient and the pharmacist. Search by receipt, patient or prescriber.
