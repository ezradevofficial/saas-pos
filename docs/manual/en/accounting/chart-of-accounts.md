The chart of accounts lists every account the company posts to.

1. Open "Chart of accounts" under "Accounting". The "Accounts" tab lists them.
2. If the company has no chart yet, choose the "Template" (the company’s country by default) and tap "Create the chart". You can add accounts afterwards.
3. To add an account, tap "Add account".
4. Type the "Code" and the "Name". Choose the "Type" and the "Subtype".
5. Choose the "Parent heading" and the "Cash-flow class" if they apply. Leave "Postable" on for accounts that take postings.
6. Tap "Save account".

To change an account, tap "Edit" on its row. To stop using one, tap "Archive"; "Restore" brings it back. The "Settings" tab sets which accounts receivables, payables, tax, exchange differences and the year-end close post to.
