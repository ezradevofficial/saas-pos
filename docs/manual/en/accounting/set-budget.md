A budget sets what you plan to earn and spend, by account and by month, for one fiscal year. Budgets are part of Accounting Pro.

1. Open "Budgets" under "Accounting" and tap "Add budget".
2. Type the "Name" and check the "Fiscal year". Tap "Add budget".
3. The budget opens on the "Budget" tab. Each row is an income or expense account; the columns "M1" to "M12" are the months.
4. Type the planned amount in each month. To budget one cost centre, choose it in "Cost centre"; "Whole company" is the default.
5. Tap "Save budget".

Open the "Against actual" tab to compare. Each row shows the "Budget", the "Actual" posted so far and the "Variance". If a range holds nothing, you see "Nothing budgeted or spent in this range."

Without the permission to manage budgets, you can read the grid but not change it.
