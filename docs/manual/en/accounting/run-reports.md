Reports show the base currency. Select a line to see its ledger.

1. Open "Reports" under "Accounting".
2. Choose a tab: "Trial balance", "Profit and loss", "Balance sheet", "Cash flow" or "Ageing". "Group" appears with Accounting Pro.
3. Set the dates. Most reports take "From" and "To"; the balance sheet and "Ageing" take "As of".
4. For "Ageing", choose whether to read customers or suppliers.
5. Check that the trial balance and the balance sheet show "Balanced".
6. Select an account line to open its general ledger and see the postings behind the figure.
