Close each month once it is complete. Nothing posts into a closed period.

1. Post or cancel every draft dated in the month.
2. Open "Periods" under "Accounting".
3. Find the month in the list and tap "Close period" on its row.
4. Read the dialog. Earlier open periods close too. For the last period of the fiscal year, income and expenses move to retained earnings.
5. If the company has Accounting Pro, tick "Revalue foreign currency items first" when you need it.
6. Confirm with "Close period".

To reopen the latest closed period, tap "Reopen", type the "Reason" and tap "Reopen". The reopening is recorded, and a year-end closing entry is reversed.
