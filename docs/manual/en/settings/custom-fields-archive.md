1. Open Settings, then "Custom fields".
2. Choose the record type with the tabs, then find the field. Search by label or key, or filter by "Type" and "Status".
3. To change it, tap "Edit". The key and type are locked, but you can change the label, help text, order, rules and who sees it. Tap "Save changes".
4. To remove it from forms and lists, tap "Archive", then "Archive field".

Archiving keeps the values already saved. Restoring the field with "Restore" shows them again. Tap "Keep field" to cancel.
