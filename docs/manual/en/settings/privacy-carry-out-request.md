Someone other than the person who logged a request approves it. A request you logged yourself shows "You logged this request, so someone else approves it."

Warning: "Anonymise" anonymises the person's details and can't be undone. Sales, invoices and other legal records are kept. Check the person's identity first.

1. Open Settings, then "Privacy".
2. On the "Data requests" tab, find a request that is "Received".
3. Tap "Approve", or tap "Reject" and write the "Reason", for example that the identity was not confirmed. The reason is kept with the request.
4. For an approved request, tap the action for its kind:
   - "Prepare report" prepares a copy of everything held about the person. Then tap "Download report".
   - "Apply corrections" changes the details entered on the request.
   - "Anonymise" anonymises the person.
5. The request moves to "Completed".
