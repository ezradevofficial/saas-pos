Tax codes carry effective-dated rates. A new rate applies from the date you choose, and the previous rate ends the day before. Never guess a rate: take it from the tax authority's official source.

1. Open Finance, then "Taxes".
2. Choose the company in the company switcher. The "Tax codes" tab lists its codes and the rate in force today.
3. If the company has no codes yet, tap "Apply country pack", then "Add missing codes". This adds the country pack's codes that the company doesn't have yet. Codes and rates you already have are never changed.
4. A code with "Rate needed" has no confirmed rate. Tap "Add rate" on it.
5. Enter the "Rate" as a percentage, and the date under "Effective from".
6. Tap "Add rate".

A zero-rated code always has a rate of 0%. Tap "Rates" on a code to see its history.
