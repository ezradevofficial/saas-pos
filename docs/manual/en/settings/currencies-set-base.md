Warning: accounts are kept in the base currency, and it locks after the first posting. Choose it carefully before the company records anything.

1. Open Finance, then "Currencies".
2. Choose the company in the company switcher, or in the list on the page.
3. In the card "Base and reporting currencies", choose the "Base currency".
4. Under "Reporting currencies", choose up to the allowed number of other currencies that reports can also show. Choose "None" to leave one empty.
5. Tap "Save currencies". The page confirms "Currencies saved."

Once amounts are posted, the card shows "Base currency locked" and the base currency can't be changed.
