The delivery log lists every notification your organisation sent, by recipient and channel. Use it to find out why someone did not receive a message.

1. Open Settings, then "Delivery log".
2. Search by recipient, address, subject or type.
3. Filter by "Status" and "Channel".
4. Read the row: who it was "Sent to", the "Status", the number of "Attempts" and the "Reason or error".

A status of "Skipped" means the person chose not to receive it on that channel or has no address for it. "Failed" shows the last error.
