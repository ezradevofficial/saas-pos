For items, customers, suppliers and employees, choose whether every company in your group uses the same records ("Shared across companies") or each company keeps its own ("Per company").

Warning: this changes who can see which records across the whole organisation. Sharing makes every company's records visible to people in any company, and codes must then be unique across the group. Check with the people who work in each company before you change it.

1. Open Master data, then "Sharing".
2. Find the kind of record and tap "Change".
3. Read the points in the window. Keeping records per company means each record belongs to one company; sharing means records stop belonging to a company.
4. If some records have no company yet and you keep them per company, choose the company that receives them under "Company that receives records with no company".
5. If you are sharing, tick the box that confirms every company's records become visible across the group.
6. Tap "Keep per company" or "Share across companies".

If two companies use the same code, sharing is refused and the page lists the codes to change first. Tap "Keep as it is" to leave things as they are.
