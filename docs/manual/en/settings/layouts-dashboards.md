Arrange the dashboard of the organisation, or of one role. A person's own copy, made with "Customise my dashboard", wins over these.

1. Open Settings, then "Dashboards".
2. Under "Applies to", choose "Everyone (default)" or one role.
3. Tap "Add widget" and choose the widget. Drag a widget to move it and drag its corner to resize it, or select it and use the arrow keys.
4. Select a widget to change its "Title", "Type" (such as "Figure", "Chart" or "List") and "Data".
5. Tap "Remove widget" to take one off.
6. Tap "Save draft" to keep your work, and tap the "Publish" button with the next version number to put it live.

People only see data they are allowed to see; a widget never gives access to more.
