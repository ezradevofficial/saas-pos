The reference rate comes from the central bank feed. A shop rate is set by your business, and tills use it when it is present. Switch on at least two currencies first (see "Add a currency and set its decimals and rounding").

1. Open Finance, then "Exchange rates".
2. Choose the company in the company switcher, then the "Currency pair".
3. Read "Reference rate" and "Shop rate" for the pair.
4. Tap "Set shop rate".
5. Enter the "Rate": how many of the second currency one unit of the first is worth. If you want, enter the "Buying rate" and "Selling rate" rates.
6. Choose "Effective now", or set "Effective from" for a later date.
7. Tap "Save shop rate".

If the new rate moved more than the allowed tolerance from the previous one, the page warns you. The rate is saved and in use, and the change is recorded for review, so check it was entered correctly. "History" lists every rate for the pair.
