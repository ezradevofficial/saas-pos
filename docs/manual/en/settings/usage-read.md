Usage is counted once a day. The Owner can see what the organisation uses against what the plan includes.

1. Open Settings, then "Usage".
2. Read "Usage against your plan": outlets, POS devices, users and employees, each as "used of allowed".
3. Each line shows a state: "Within plan", "Over plan", "Adding paused" or "No limit". Over the plan, the page shows how far over and the "Extra cost".
4. When adding more is paused, tap "Upgrade" to change your plan.

Everything already set up keeps working. Sales, payroll and approvals are never stopped.
