Build the forms your organisation needs, such as a petty cash request: their fields, lines, attachments, numbering and approval.

1. Open Settings, then "Forms".
2. Tap "New form".
3. Type the "Name" as people will see it, for example Petty cash request. Check the "Key"; it can't change later.
4. Add a "Description" if it helps.
5. Switch on "Send each form for approval" if each form must be approved, "Add a table of lines" if it holds a table of lines, and "Allow attached files" if people may attach files.
6. Under "Roles that use this form", choose the roles that use the form. Leave it empty for everyone who may use forms. Owners always can.
7. Tap "Create form".
8. Open the form from the list to set up the rest: "Fields" and "Line fields" (these are custom fields), "Form layout", "Numbering" and "Approval flow".

Use "Change" to change a form later and "Archive" to retire it.
