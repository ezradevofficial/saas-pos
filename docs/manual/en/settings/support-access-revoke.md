You can end an approved support session at any time. Revoking ends it at once.

1. Open Settings, then "Support access".
2. Under "Open now", find the session.
3. Tap "Revoke" to end it.
4. To see what the session did, tap "See activity". The list shows when, what and the result of every request.

While a session is open, a banner reads "Support access active". Tap "Review or revoke" in it to come here.
