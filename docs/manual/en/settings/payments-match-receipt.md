Mobile money requests from the tills show under "Payment requests". Money received that matched no request shows under "Unmatched receipts", for example when a customer paid with the wrong account.

1. Open Finance, then "Payments received".
2. Choose the company in the company switcher.
3. To follow a request, stay on "Payment requests". Search by reference or M-Pesa code, and filter by status. A request shows "Paid", "Failed", "Timed out" or another status.
4. To settle a receipt, open "Unmatched receipts". The filter "Not matched" shows what is left.
5. Tap "Match to payment" on the receipt.
6. Under "Payment request", choose the payment request it settles, then tap "Match payment".

If no request is waiting, the window says so. Codes typed at the till and phone prompts without an answer appear there.
