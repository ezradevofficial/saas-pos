Change the texts your organisation sends, per notification and channel. The text is written once, in your organisation's language, and everyone receives it as written.

1. Open Settings, then "Notification templates".
2. Under "Notification types", choose the notification.
3. Choose the "Channel".
4. Write the "Subject" (email) and the "Message". Tap a placeholder under "Placeholders" to insert it where the cursor is. Use only that notification's placeholders.
5. Check "Preview with sample values".
6. Tap "Save template".

To go back to the built-in text, tap "Use default" and confirm with "Use default". Everyone then receives the default text in their own language.

With the right to edit notification settings, you can also choose "Required channels": channels users can't switch off for that notification.
