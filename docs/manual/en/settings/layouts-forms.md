Arrange the fields of an item, customer, supplier or custom form into sections, tabs and columns, for the organisation or one role. Hiding a field here only tidies the form; it doesn't remove the field or its permissions.

1. Open Settings, then "Form layouts".
2. Choose the "Form" and who the layout is for under "Applies to".
3. Under "Sections", drag fields to reorder them or to move them to another section. Use "Add section" to add a section and set its "Section title" and "Columns".
4. To rename a field, change its help text or hide it from some roles, use the change button on the field, make the changes and tap "Apply".
5. To hide a field, use the hide button on the field. Hidden fields wait under "Hidden fields"; tap "Place" to show one again. A required field with no default can't be hidden.
6. Use "Add tab" to group sections into tabs.
7. Check the "Preview", tap "Save draft", and tap the "Publish" button with the next version number.
