Set how the sell screen looks on your tills: the product grid, category tiles, quick buttons, where the sale panel sits and what the customer display shows.

1. Open Settings, then "POS layout".
2. Under "Layout for", choose the whole business, a company, a branch or a location. A location's layout wins over its branch's, a branch's over its company's.
3. Under "Product grid", set the columns on a tablet and a phone and the "Tile size".
4. Under "Products", pin items so they come first, and choose the "Order after pinned items" for the rest: "By category", "By name" or "Best-sellers".
5. Under "Category tiles", drag category tiles to order them, and set a colour or hide a category.
6. Under "Quick buttons", add up to the maximum number of buttons above the grid: an item, a category or an action such as "Hold sale".
7. Under "Sale panel", choose the side the sale panel sits on a tablet.
8. Under "Customer display", write the "Welcome text" and choose what customers see.
9. Check the "Preview" on tablet and phone, then save the draft and tap the "Publish" button with the next version number.

Touch targets stay at least 48 px. Tills pick up the new layout the next time they sync.
