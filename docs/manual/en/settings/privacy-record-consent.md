Record whether a customer agrees to marketing messages, by channel, with what they said as evidence.

1. Open Settings, then "Privacy".
2. Open the "Marketing consent" tab.
3. Find the customer by typing part of their name.
4. Read the "Current answer" answer: "Agreed", "Withdrawn" or "Not asked".
5. Under "Record an answer", choose the "Channel": "Email", "SMS", "WhatsApp" or "All channels".
6. Write "What the customer said", for example where and how they answered.
7. Tap "Record agreement" or "Record withdrawal".

"History" keeps every answer. Do not send marketing on a channel where the customer withdrew.
