Choose how each kind of notification reaches you.

1. Open Settings, then "Notifications".
2. For each notification, tick the channels you want: in-app, email, push, SMS or WhatsApp. A channel marked "Required by your organisation" can't be switched off.
3. Under "Email timing", choose "Immediately", "Daily digest" or "Weekly digest". A notification your organisation requires by email arrives at once.
4. Tap "Save changes". The page confirms "Notification settings saved."

Tap "Discard changes" to undo changes you have not saved.
