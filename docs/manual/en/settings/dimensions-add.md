Dimensions tag transactions so you can see income and costs by department, cost centre or project.

1. Open Master data, then "Dimensions".
2. Choose the company in the company switcher.
3. Open the tab you need: "Departments", "Cost centres" or "Projects".
4. Tap the add button of the tab, such as "Add cost centre".
5. Type the "Code" (letters, numbers, dots, hyphens and underscores, up to 30 characters) and the "Name".
6. Choose a "Parent" to place it under another record, or leave it at top level.
7. Choose the "Owner". Approvals go to this person.
8. Save.

To retire one, tap "Archive" on its row. It can't be used on new transactions, past transactions keep it, and "Restore" brings it back. A record with active records under it can't be archived until those are moved or archived.
