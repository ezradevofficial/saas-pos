Design how receipts, invoices and other documents print. Printed documents are black on white. Tax authority details and tax lines always print where the law requires them, and their blocks are locked.

1. Open Settings, then "Document templates".
2. Under "Document types", open the document type.
3. Under "Applies to", choose whether the template applies to the whole organisation, a company or a branch. A branch template wins over its company's, and a company's over the organisation's.
4. Add blocks from "Add a block", such as "Text", "Logo", "Lines table" or "QR code". Drag a block to move it, or select it and use the arrow keys.
5. Select a block to change it under "Block settings".
6. Under "Page", choose the "Paper", the "Language" and the margins.
7. Check the "Preview", which uses sample data until the module is set up. Tap "Download PDF" to see it as a file.
8. Tap "Save draft" to keep your work without changing live documents.
9. When the page shows no problems, tap the "Publish" button with the next version number.

If a locked tax authority or totals block is missing, publishing is blocked. Tap "Add the blocks back". To print different layouts for different documents, add a variant under "Variants".
