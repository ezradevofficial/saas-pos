Rename, reorder, group and hide menu items for the organisation or a role, and set the page the role starts on. Hiding an item only tidies the menu. Permissions still decide what a person can open.

1. Open Settings, then "Menus".
2. Choose who the menu is for under "Applies to".
3. Under "Menu", drag groups and items by their handles to reorder them, or focus a handle, press Space and use the arrow keys.
4. Type a new name in the name field of an item or group. Use "Add group" to add a group and the group field of an item to move it into one.
5. Use the show and hide control of an item to take it off the menu.
6. Under "Home page", choose the page people start on.
7. Check the "Preview", tap "Save draft", and tap the "Publish" button with the next version number.
