Apps connect to your organisation through the public API. Each one asks for what it needs, and you choose the role it acts as.

Warning: an installed app can read and change your data, within the role you give it, and may receive events about your business. Install only apps you trust, and give each the smallest role that works. A paid app is added to your subscription.

1. Open Settings, then "Marketplace".
2. Search by name or choose a "Category". Open an app to read "About", "What it can do", "Events it receives" and the "Price".
3. Tap "Install". The app sends you back to approve what it asks for.
4. Read "It will be able to" and the events it will receive.
5. Under "It acts as", choose the role and where it applies. The app can never do more than this role allows.
6. Tap "Approve and install", or "Deny" to refuse.

An app that is not set up yet shows "Not set up yet" and can't be installed until the platform team configures it.
