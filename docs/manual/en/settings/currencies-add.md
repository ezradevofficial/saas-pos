Switched-on currencies can be used by every company for prices, payments and rates.

1. Open Finance, then "Currencies".
2. Tap "Add currency".
3. Under "Choose a currency", pick the currency, then tap "Add currency".
4. To change a currency, tap "Edit" on its row. Set the "Decimals" and the "Cash rounding": cash totals round to a multiple of this amount, for example CDF 50 or KES 1.00.
5. Use the switch on the row to turn a currency on or off. "Off" stops it from being used for new prices and payments.

Decimals lock once amounts are stored in the currency. If you change the decimals, the cash rounding clears and you enter it again.
