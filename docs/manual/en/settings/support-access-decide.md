Our support team sees nothing of your business unless you approve a request. Each request names who asks, why, and for how many hours.

1. Open Settings, then "Support access".
2. Under "Requests waiting for you", read the reason, the access asked ("Read-only" or "Read and write") and the hours.
3. Tap "Review and approve" to approve, or "Deny" to refuse.
4. In the approval window, decide whether to switch on "Include payroll, HR and payment data". It is off unless you tick it: pay, bank details, medical and disciplinary records, payments and payment methods stay hidden otherwise.
5. Confirm the approval for the hours shown.

Approved sessions show under "Open now" until they end. "History" keeps every earlier request.
