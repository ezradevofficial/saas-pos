Measure a tank and record the dip. The litres come from the tank's chart.

1. Open a shift first. Dips are sent with the shift.
2. On "Forecourt", tap "Record a dip".
3. Choose the "Tank".
4. Type the "Dip" in cm. Add "Water (optional)" if you measured it.
5. Tap "Record dip". The tank and its litres show as confirmation.

If the dip is outside the tank's chart, measure again. If the tank has no chart yet, ask a manager to add one.
