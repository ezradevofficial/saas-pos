1. Open "Gift cards and vouchers" and tap "Check a balance".
2. Type the "Card code" and tap "Check balance". If no card has this code, check it and try again.
3. The balance shows. Tap "Open card" to see its history.

The list also shows each card's "Value", "Balance" and "Expires" date, and "Expired" for cards past it.
