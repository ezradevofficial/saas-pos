See who owes what, and for how long.

1. Open "Debt ageing". It lists what customers owe by the age of each sale on account: "0–30 days", "31–60 days", "61–90 days" and "Over 90 days", with the total "Open" (owed).
2. Change the date under "As of" to see the position on another day. Use "Search customers" to find one.
3. Show the "Credit limit" column from the column menu if you need it.

Payments settle the oldest sale first. If nobody owes anything, the page says "No customer owes anything."
