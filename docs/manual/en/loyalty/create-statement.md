1. Open "Credit accounts" and tap the customer's account.
2. Tap "Statement".
3. Set the dates "From" and "To", and tap "Create statement".
4. The statement appears under "Statements" with its "Number", "Period" and "Closing balance". Tap "Download PDF" to print or send it.
