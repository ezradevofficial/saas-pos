1. Open "Gift cards and vouchers" and tap "Issue cards".
2. Choose the "Kind": gift card or voucher. Type the "Value", "How many" and, if it ends, "Expires on".
3. Switch on "Load them now (a voucher given, or cards sold here)" for vouchers you give away. Leave it off for cards you will sell: they stay "Not sold" until paid.
4. Tap "Issue <count>".
5. "Codes issued" lists the codes. "Print or copy these codes now. They are not shown again." Tap "Done" when you have them.

Afterwards a card is found by its last four characters: use "Search by the last four characters".
