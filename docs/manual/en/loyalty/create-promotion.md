A promotion is a discount the tills apply on their own, offline too.

1. Open "Promotions" and tap "New promotion".
2. Under "What it gives", type the "Name" and choose the "Type": percentage off, amount off, buy X get Y or bundle price. Then fill in what the type needs: "Discount", "Amount off", "Buy (units)" and "Get (units)", or "Bundle price". Set the "Priority" (higher first; a line gets one promotion) and the "Minimum spend".
3. Under "On what and for whom", choose the "Items" and "Categories", or leave them empty for everything. Choose the "Branches" and type "Customer groups" if it is limited.
4. Under "When", set "From" and "Until", and "Daily from", "Daily until" and "Days" for a happy hour.
5. Switch on "Needs a coupon code" if customers must give a code.
6. Tap "Create and activate", or "Save as draft" to finish later.

Tills get the promotion with their next sync.
