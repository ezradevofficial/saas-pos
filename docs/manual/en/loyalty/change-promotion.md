1. Open "Promotions". Search by name or filter by status ("Draft", "Active", "Archived").
2. Tap the promotion. It shows its "Version".
3. To change it, edit the fields and tap "Save changes". It saves as a new version and tills get it with their next sync.
4. A draft is not applied until you tap "Activate".
5. To stop a promotion, tap "Archive". An archived promotion can no longer be edited.

Use the "Preview" card to check a change. See "Add coupon codes and preview a promotion".
