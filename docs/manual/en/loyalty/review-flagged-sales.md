Some till sales are kept with something to check: points or gift cards spent offline, coupons, promotions, credit.

1. Open "Sales to review". Each row shows the "Receipt", when it was "Sold at", the "Customer", the "Total" and the reasons under "To review".
2. Use "Search by receipt", or the "All reasons" filter to look at one reason, for example "Over the credit limit" or "Coupon used too often".
3. Check the sale with the customer or the cashier and correct the account where needed. See "Record a credit payment, charge or adjustment".

If there is nothing to check, the page says "No sales to review."
