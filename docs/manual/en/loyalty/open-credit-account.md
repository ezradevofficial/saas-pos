A credit account lets a customer buy on account, up to the credit limit on their customer record.

1. Open "Credit accounts" and tap "Open credit account".
2. Under "Choose a customer", pick the customer and tap "Open credit account".
3. The list shows what each customer "Owes" and what is "Left to spend". Use "Search customers" and the status filter ("Open", "Closed").
4. To close an account, open it and tap "Close account".

To raise the limit, open the account and tap "Request a new limit". Limits change through a request that is approved.
