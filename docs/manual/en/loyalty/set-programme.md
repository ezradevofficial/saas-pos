Decide how customers earn and spend points, and when to be warned about credit and gift cards.

1. Open "Loyalty programme". Choose the company in the switcher if you have several.
2. Under "Points", switch on "Customers earn and redeem points".
3. Set "Points earned": the number of points for every amount spent ("For every"). Set what "One point is worth" and the "Least points redeemed at once".
4. Under "Expiry", set "Points expire after (months)" (leave empty so points never expire) and "Warn this many days before".
5. Under "Credit and gift cards", set the share of the credit limit at which to warn, and the balance under which a gift card is "low".
6. Tap "Save programme".

Points are earned and valued in the company's base currency.
