Labels carry the item name, code, price and a barcode, printed through the label template.

1. Open "Labels" under "Inventory".
2. Under "Price", choose the "Company" and the "Price list" the printed price comes from. "The company's default prices" is the default.
3. Under "Labels to print", on "Label 1", choose the "Item". Choose its "Unit" (the "Base unit" by default) and, if the item is tracked by batch, the "Batch". "No batch" leaves it out.
4. Type the "Copies" you need.
5. Tap "Add item" for each further label.
6. Tap "Print labels" to open the labels ready to print, or "Download PDF" to keep a file.

To drop a label, tap "Remove line" on it. For labels on fixed assets, see "Print asset labels".
