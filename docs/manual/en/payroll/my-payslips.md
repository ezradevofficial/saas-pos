"My payslips" lists your payslips from posted payroll runs. You see only your own.

1. Open "My payslips" under "My HR".
2. Tap a payslip to read it.
3. Tap "Download PDF" to save it. Tap "Back to my payslips" to return to the list.

A payslip appears once the run that pays you is posted. If the list says "No payslips yet", the run has not been posted. If the page says "Your account is not linked to an employee record", ask HR to link your account to your employee record. See "Use your self-service pages".
