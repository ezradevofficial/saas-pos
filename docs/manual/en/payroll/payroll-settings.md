Payroll settings are kept per company. Set them before the first run: the pay groups, the pay items and how overtime counts. Statutory rates are read here, never typed.

1. Open "Settings" under "Payroll". If there are several companies, choose the company first.
2. A banner may say "Statutory rates are not confirmed yet" or "There is no payroll pack for" your country. Runs for the company are refused until the platform team confirms the missing values. Tap "Review statutory rates" to see them.

Company settings

1. Open the "Company settings" tab.
2. Under "Multipliers", type the "Overtime multiplier", the "Night multiplier", the "Public holiday multiplier" and the "Rest day multiplier". Leave one empty when that time is not paid extra.
3. Under "Standard month", type the "Hours in a month" and the "Days in a month". They give the hourly and daily rate of a monthly salary.
4. Under "Overtime and premiums", choose whether they are "Taxable" and "Subject to statutory contributions".
5. Tap "Save settings".

Pay groups

1. Open the "Pay groups" tab and tap "Add pay group".
2. Type the "Code" and the "Name", and choose the "Frequency": "Monthly", "Weekly" or "Every two weeks".
3. For monthly pay, type the "Pay day", or leave it empty to pay on the last day of the month. For weekly or two-weekly pay, set "First period starts on".
4. Tap "Save pay group". Tap "Edit" to change it, or "Archive" to stop new runs for it; its runs stay as they are.

Pay items

1. Open the "Pay items" tab and tap "Add pay item".
2. Type the "Code" and the "Name", and choose the "Kind": "Earning" or "Deduction".
3. Under "How it counts", tick what applies: "Taxable", "Contributory", "Before tax" or "Insurance premium". For a deduction before tax, choose the "Statutory limit" if the country has one.
4. Tap "Save pay item". An archived item can no longer be given to employees; those who have it keep it until it ends.

The "Statutory rates" tab lists the country pack’s components in force, each with its "Value status". A value "Still to confirm" is left out of runs until it is confirmed. If the company does not owe a component, tap "Mark not applicable", type the reason (for example the exemption reference) and confirm. The reason is kept in the audit log. Tap "Apply it again" to undo it.
