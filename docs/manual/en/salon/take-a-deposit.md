A deposit holds the booking and is used when the client pays at the till.

1. Open the appointment on "Calendar". "Deposit required" shows what the salon asks for.
2. Tap "Take deposit".
3. Choose the "Payment method" and type the "Amount".
4. For an M-Pesa request, fill in the "M-Pesa phone" and tap "Send request". The deposit is held once the client pays.
5. To record a receipt typed by hand, leave the phone empty, add a "Reference" and tap "Take deposit".

A refund needs the right to refund deposits, which a manager has. At the till, the client pays with Salon credit to use the deposit first.
