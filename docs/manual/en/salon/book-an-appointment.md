Book a client with a stylist on the calendar.

1. Open "Calendar". It shows each staff member's day. Use "Previous day" and "Next day" to change the day.
2. Tap "New booking", or "Book with <name>" for a person.
3. Under "Find a client", type two letters of a name or phone number and choose the "Client". Leave it empty for "A guest with no account" and type the name and phone.
4. Choose the "Services", the "Staff member" or "Anyone free", and the "Date".
5. Pick one of the "Open times", add a "Note" if needed, and tap "Book appointment".

To take a deposit, open the appointment and tap "Take deposit". To move or cancel it, tap "Move" or "Cancel booking". A cancellation inside the window may carry a fee.
