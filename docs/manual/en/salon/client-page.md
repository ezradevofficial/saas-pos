A client page gathers what the salon knows about one person: visits, spend, deposits, preferences and notes.

1. Open "Clients" under "Salon and services". Search by name, phone or email.
2. To add someone, tap "Add a client", fill in the name, phone and "Email", and tap "Add a client".
3. Tap a row, or "Open", to open the client page.
4. Read the figures at the top: "Visits", "Spent", "Deposits held" and, if the client bought a package, "Prepaid value left". The line below shows the last visit, the next booking, and any no-shows or cancellations.
5. Under "Profile", read the "Preferred staff member" and the "Preferences". "Allergies" and "Formulas" show only to people who may read client notes; they are private.
6. Under "Visits", read each visit, its total and the "Formula used".
7. To change the record, tap "Edit profile", then "Save client". To book the client again, tap "Book again". It opens the new appointment page with the client chosen. See "Book an appointment on the full page".

A client with allergies or formulas on file shows a mark in the list so the stylist checks before the visit.
