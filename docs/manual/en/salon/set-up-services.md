List what the salon sells by appointment: how long it takes, who does it, which chair it needs and what it uses.

1. Open "Service menu" and tap "Add a service".
2. Choose the "Item", the "Category" and the "Type" (service or add-on).
3. Type the "Duration (minutes)" and the "Clean-up (minutes)" kept free after it. The "Price" comes from the item.
4. Under "Staff who do it" and "Chairs or rooms", tick who and what can do or host it. Under "Add-ons offered", tick the add-ons that go with it.
5. Under "Products used", tap "Add a product", choose the "Product" and its "Quantity".
6. Switch "Clients can book this online" if the service appears on the booking page. Tap "Save service".
7. To change one, tap "Edit" on its row. To take it off the menu, tap "Archive". Past appointments keep it.
