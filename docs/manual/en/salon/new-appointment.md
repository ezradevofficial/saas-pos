The "New appointment" page books a client in one go: the client, the services and time, then a note and an optional deposit. It opens from "Book again" on a client page and from a search for "Book an appointment".

1. Under "Client", type two letters of a name or phone number. Tap the match, or tap "Add a new client" if the person is not on file. To change your choice, tap "Change client".
2. For a guest with no client record, leave the search empty and type a "Name" and a "Phone".
3. Under "Services and time", pick the "Services" first. "Staff member" then lists only the staff who do all of them; leave "Anyone free" to let the salon choose.
4. Choose the "Date". The "Open times" appear. Tap one. A line under them names each service, stylist and time.
5. Under "Note and deposit", add a "Note" if needed. If you may take deposits, choose "Deposit paid with" and the "Deposit amount". Leave "No deposit now" to skip it.
6. Tap "Book at" followed by the time. Tap "Cancel" to go back to the calendar.

If the booking is saved but the deposit fails, the page says so; take the deposit from the appointment. See "Take a deposit on a booking".
