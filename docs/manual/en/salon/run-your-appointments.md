The till lists the day's appointments. Move each one from arrival to payment.

1. Open the till menu and choose "Appointments". Pick the "Day", and under "Staff" yourself or "Everyone" if your role allows.
2. If an appointment shows "Notes to read", this client has notes or allergies. Read them before you start.
3. When the client arrives, tap "Check in".
4. Tap "Start service" when you begin and "Finish service" when you are done.
5. Tap "Charge". Choose a tip or "No tip", then tap "Charge <amount>" and take the payment. A deposit held shows under "Deposit available".
6. If the client did not come, tap "Mark as no-show". If they cancel, tap "Cancel appointment".

If "Fee due" shows, tap "Charge the fee <amount>". Appointments you do offline are sent when you are back online.
