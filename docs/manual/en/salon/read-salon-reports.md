Five tabs show how busy staff are, what services earn, who does not turn up and what the salon owes.

1. Open "Salon reports". Set "From" and "To".
2. "Utilisation" shows, per staff member, the minutes "Available", "Booked" and "Served", and the utilisation rate.
3. "Services" shows what each service earned: "Done", "Revenue" and "At list price".
4. "No-shows" shows "Kept", "No-shows", "Cancellations" and the "No-show rate", with a split per staff member.
5. "Commissions" shows what staff "Earned", what was "Reversed" and the "Net", with tips.
6. "Prepaid value" shows the package value and sessions left, what expires within 30 days, what expired and the deposits held.
