Set the diary, the fees, the deposit and the online booking page. Only the Owner can change them.

1. Open "Salon settings". Choose the company if asked.
2. Under "Diary", choose the "Time step" and the "Reminder (hours before)".
3. Under "Cancellation and no-show fees", set "Free cancellation until (hours before)", the "Late cancellation fee" and the "No-show fee".
4. Under "Deposits", set the "Deposit" percent. Switch "Online bookings must be paid with a deposit" if you want it, and set "Hold an unpaid online booking (minutes)".
5. Under "At the till", choose the "Tip item" and the "Fee item" and type the "Payroll commission code".
6. Under "Privacy", type "Keep guest details for (days)".
7. Tap "Save settings".
8. Under "Online booking", switch "Accept online bookings", set the "Name shown", the "Earliest booking (minutes ahead)" and "Latest booking (days ahead)", and choose the "Salon floor". Copy the "Public link". Tap "Save booking page". "New link" replaces the link and the old one stops working.
