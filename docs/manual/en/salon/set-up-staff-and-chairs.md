Say who works at the salon, when, and which chairs and rooms they use.

1. Open "Staff and chairs" and the tab "Staff". Tap "Add a staff member".
2. Type the name, the "Title" and choose a "Colour" for the calendar. Choose the "Home branch" and, if they use {{app_name}}, "Signs in as".
3. Switch "Clients can book them online" if clients may pick them. Tap "Save staff member".
4. On the staff row, tap "Hours". Add a span for each "Day" with its "Type" ("Work" or "Break") and "From" and "To" times. Tap "Save hours".
5. Tap "Time off" to record leave.
6. Open the tab "Chairs and rooms". Tap "Add a chair or room", name it, choose its "Kind" and save.
7. To remove a person or chair from new bookings, tap "Archive". Past appointments keep them.
