This page is for your clients, not your staff. It needs no account. You turn it on in the salon settings under "Online booking" ("Accept online bookings"), where the "Public link" is shown. Share that link or put it on your website. "New link" replaces it; the old one stops working.

What a client sees:

1. The page opens with "Book an appointment" and your branch name (or the "Name shown" you set).
2. "Choose your services", then "With": a stylist or anyone free.
3. "Open times": only times that respect your earliest and latest booking limits.
4. "Your details": a name and a phone number or email. The page says only these are kept, to confirm and remind.
5. The client taps "Book at" and a time. The page then says "Your appointment is booked" with a "Reference" and the "Total", and promises a reminder.
6. If you ask for a deposit, the page says so beforehand. After booking the client enters the "M-Pesa phone", taps "Pay deposit" and approves on the phone. "Check payment" refreshes the result.

If the code is wrong or online booking is off, the page says "This booking page is not available." The booking then appears on your calendar like any other. See "Work through your appointments".
