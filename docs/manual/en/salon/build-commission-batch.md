Commissions are what staff earned on paid visits. A batch is reviewed and approved before it goes to payroll.

1. Open "Commissions". "Batches" lists the batches; "Ledger" and "Tips" list the entries.
2. Tap "Build a batch". Choose the "Currency" and the end date. It takes every commission not yet in a batch.
3. Open the batch with "Open". Read each staff member's "Payouts" and the "Entries".
4. Tap "Submit for approval".
5. Another manager opens the batch and taps "Approve", or "Reject" with a reason. Whoever built or submitted a batch, or is paid in it, cannot approve it.
6. After approval, tap "Download CSV" under "Export".

The commission rules themselves belong to the Admin; your role cannot change them.
