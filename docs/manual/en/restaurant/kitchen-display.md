The kitchen display shows the tickets sent to your station, oldest first.

1. Open "Kitchen display". Pick your "Location" and your "Station".
2. A new ticket shows its table or tab, its course and how many minutes it has waited. Re-sends and voids carry a "Resend" or "Void" mark.
3. Tap "Start" when you begin the ticket.
4. Tap "Mark ready" when it is done. The waiter then marks it served.
5. If you moved a ticket by mistake, tap "Recall".

If "The display could not refresh", it tries again in a few seconds. Tap "Leave the display" to go back to the app.
