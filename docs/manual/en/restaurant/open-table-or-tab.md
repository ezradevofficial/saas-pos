Open an order for a table in the dining room, or a tab for a guest at the bar.

1. Open the till menu and choose "Tables".
2. Under "View", choose "Tables" or "Tabs". Under "Show", choose "Mine" to see only your own, or "All" to see the whole floor.
3. For a table, tap a free table. On "Open <table>", set "Guests" with "More guests" and "Fewer guests", then tap "Open table".
4. For a tab, tap "New tab". Type a "Tab name", add a "Customer (optional)" if you wish, then tap "Open tab".

A table that is also open on another till shows a warning: check with the other waiter before you send. For a sale without a table, choose "Quick sale" in the till menu.
