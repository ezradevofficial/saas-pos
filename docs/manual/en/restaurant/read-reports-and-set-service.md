See how the restaurant traded, and set the service charge and the tips the till offers.

1. Open "Restaurant reports". Choose the location and set "From" and "To".
2. Read "Guests", "Orders", "Sales", "Tips", "Average spend per guest" and "Average table time". "Sales by waiter" lists each waiter. "Voids after send" lists who removed sent items, how many, their value and the reasons.
3. Open "Restaurant settings". Choose the company if asked.
4. Under "Service charge and tips", type the "Service charge" as a percent (not more than 30) and choose the "Service charge item" and the "Tip item".
5. Type the "Tip presets (percent)" as percentages from 1 to 100 separated by commas.
6. Tap "Save settings".

Only the Owner can change the settings. Others can read them.
