"Sessions" lists the devices where you are signed in. End any session you do not recognise.

1. Open Settings, then "Sessions".
2. Read each row: "Device", "IP address" and "Last active". Your current one is marked "This device".
3. Tap "Sign out" beside a session you do not recognise.
4. Confirm. The other sessions stay signed in.

If you think someone else has your password, end the sessions, then change your password with "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page. The reset signs you out everywhere.

To end every session of another person, open them under Settings, Users and tap "Sign out everywhere".
