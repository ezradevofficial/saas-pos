These rules apply to everyone in your organisation.

1. Open Settings, then "Security".
2. Set the "Minimum password length": 8 to 64 characters. It applies the next time someone sets a password.
3. Set the "Session timeout": 15 to 480 minutes without activity before someone is signed out.
4. Choose the "Default language". It is used for emails and for people who have not chosen a language.
5. Tap "Save changes". The page confirms "Settings saved."
