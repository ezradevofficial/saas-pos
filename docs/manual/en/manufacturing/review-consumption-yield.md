Two reports compare what the orders used and made with the standard.

1. Open "Consumption" under "Manufacturing". For each order and material, compare the "Standard" with the "Actual", the "Difference" and "Difference %". With cost rights you also see the "Actual value", the "Standard value" and the "Value difference".
2. Open "Yield and variances" under "Manufacturing". For each order, read the good output, the scrap, "Yield %" and "Scrap %", and the costs.
3. In both, use "Search by order number" and the filters "Status", location and the "From" and "To" dates.
4. Tap "Export" and choose "Excel (.xlsx)", "CSV" or "PDF" to take the rows away.

A large difference tells you to check the BOM’s quantities and wastage, or how materials are issued.
