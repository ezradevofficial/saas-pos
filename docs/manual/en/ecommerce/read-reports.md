See orders, revenue and deliveries for a period.

1. Open "Online reports".
2. Choose the "Store" (or all stores), and the dates "From" and "To".
3. Read the "Revenue" and "Deliveries" figures, "Orders by channel", "Orders by payment", the "Top products" and "Cash not handed in yet".

If there is nothing in the period, the page says "No orders in this period."
