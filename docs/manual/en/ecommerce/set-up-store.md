Create the store customers order from.

1. Open "Store settings" and tap "New store".
2. Type the "Store name" and the "Web address": lower-case letters, digits and dashes. The store opens at /shop/ followed by it.
3. Choose the "Branch", the "Fulfilment location" where orders are packed, the "Currency" and the "Price list". Set the "Minimum order" if you have one.
4. Set "Hold stock for unpaid orders (minutes)" and the "Safety buffer (units)".
5. Add the "Contact phone", "Contact email" and "WhatsApp number" customers see. Switch "Delivery" and "Pickup" on or off, and choose the "Pickup point".
6. Under "Payment methods", choose the method that takes each kind of payment.
7. Tap "Create store". Later, tap "Save store" to keep changes.

Set the "Status" to "Live" when the store is ready. "Draft" and "Paused" keep it closed. Then add products and areas: see "Add products to your online store" and "Set delivery areas and fees".
