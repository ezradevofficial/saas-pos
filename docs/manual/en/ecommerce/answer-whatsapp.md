Reply to messages customers send to your store on WhatsApp.

1. Open "WhatsApp inbox". The "Conversations" list shows each customer.
2. Tap a conversation to read the "Messages". A message that failed shows "Not sent".
3. Type your answer under "Reply" and tap "Send".
4. When the chat is finished, tap "Close chat".

The inbox is empty ("No conversations.") until the store's WhatsApp is connected. See "Share your store and connect WhatsApp".
