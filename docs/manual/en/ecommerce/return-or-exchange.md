Record items a customer sends back, or swaps for others.

1. Open the delivered order and tap "Return or exchange". To take back the whole order, tap "Mark returned" instead (available while it is out for delivery or delivered).
2. Under "Items coming back", enter the quantity of each item. A line shows "Up to <qty>".
3. Choose a reason.
4. To swap, tick "Exchange for another item", choose the "Product" and "Quantity". Tap "Add a product" for more, or "Remove this item".
5. The "Amounts" card shows the "Value returned", the "Value of the exchange" and the "Difference".
6. Tap "Record return", "Record exchange", or the refund or collect button shown with the amount.

Afterwards the order lists "Returns and exchanges". Where money is due, tap "Refund" (to pay back) or "Collect" (to take the difference) beside it. For a collection, choose the "Payment method" and type the "Payment reference".
