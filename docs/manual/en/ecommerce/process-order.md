Take an order from the online store, the phone or WhatsApp to the customer's hands.

1. Open "Online orders". Use the filters for status, payment, channel, fulfilment and store, or "Search by number, name or phone". Tap the order.
2. Check "Items", the "Address" or "Pickup point", and "Check this order" if the page shows flags.
3. Tap "Accept". It is available once the order is paid or marked "Cash on delivery".
4. Tap "Start picking" while you pack it, then "Mark ready".
5. For delivery, assign a rider (see "Assign a rider to a delivery"), then tap "Hand to rider".
6. When the customer has the goods, tap "Mark delivered". For a cash on delivery order, type the "Cash collected" if it differs from the amount due.
7. For a pickup order, tap "Mark delivered" when the customer collects.

The "History" card lists every step.
