Tell customers where you deliver and what it costs.

1. Open "Store settings" and the "Delivery areas" tab.
2. Under "Add area", type the "Area name" and the "Delivery fee". Optionally set "Free delivery from" and the "Delivery time".
3. Tap "Add area". It appears in the list with its "Fee", "Free from" and "Delivery time".
4. To stop delivering to an area, tap "Remove <name>".

Customers choose one of these areas at checkout. With no areas, there is no delivery: "No delivery areas yet."
