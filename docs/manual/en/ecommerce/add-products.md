Choose which items the store sells.

1. Open "Store settings" and the "Catalogue" tab. If you have several stores, choose the "Store" first.
2. Under "Add items", search with "Search items by name or code" and tap an item to add it. It shows "Added to the catalogue."
3. In the "Catalogue" table, use "Published" to show or hide a product, and "Featured" to put it forward. "Stock" shows how many are available.
4. To take a product out, tap "Remove <name>".

Items must exist in Inventory first. See "Add an item with its units".
