Give customers the link and receive their WhatsApp messages in the inbox.

1. Open "Store settings" and the "Links and WhatsApp" tab.
2. Under "Store link", tap "Copy link" or "Share on WhatsApp".
3. Under "WhatsApp messages", tap "Connect WhatsApp". A web address appears: "Give this address to your WhatsApp provider. It is shown only once." Copy it into your provider's settings.
4. When connected, the card says "Messages from your WhatsApp provider arrive in the inbox." To make a new address, tap "Replace the WhatsApp address".

The "WhatsApp order messages" card shows whether order updates by WhatsApp are available for your account.
