Customers use the store without signing in to {{app_name}}. Knowing the steps helps you support them.

1. The customer opens the store link. They can "Search products", choose a category under "Categories", and open a product.
2. On a product, they choose the "Quantity" and tap "Add to cart". A product that is not available shows "Out of stock".
3. In the "Cart" they use "One more" and "One less", then tap "Go to checkout".
4. At "Checkout" they choose "Delivery or pickup", the "Delivery area" or "Pickup point", and fill in "Name", "Phone", "Email" and the "Address". They can enter a "Coupon code" and choose to "Send order updates on WhatsApp".
5. They choose the "Payment" and tap "Place order and pay <amount>".
6. The order page "Order <number>" shows the "Progress", the rider and the rider's last position once on the way. If the payment fails they can tap "Pay again".

Under "Account", customers sign in with a 6-digit code sent to their email, see "Your orders", and turn "Offers and news" on or off at any time.

The order then appears in "Online orders". See "Process an online order".
