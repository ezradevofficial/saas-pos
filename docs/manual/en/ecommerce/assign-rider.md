Give each delivery to a rider before it leaves.

1. Open "Deliveries". The columns are "No rider yet", "Assigned" and "On the way". Each card shows the order, the address and the cash to "Collect".
2. On a card in "No rider yet" or "Assigned", choose the rider under "Choose a rider" and tap "Assign".
3. You can do the same from the order: open it, choose the "Rider" and tap "Assign".
4. The order's "Hand to rider" button works once a rider is set.

When a delivery is on the way, tap "Track" to follow it. See "Track a delivery".
