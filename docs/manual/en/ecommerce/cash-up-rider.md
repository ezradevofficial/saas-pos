Close the cash a rider collected on delivery and hands in.

1. Open "Rider cash-ups". "Waiting to be handed in" lists each rider with the cash they collected.
2. Count the cash. On the rider's row, tap "Cash up".
3. Type the "Cash counted". Add a "Note" if it differs from what was expected.
4. Tap "Close the cash-up".

The cash-up moves to "Closed cash-ups". When nothing is waiting, the page says "No cash is waiting."
