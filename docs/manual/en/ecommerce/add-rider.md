Riders deliver online orders. Each signs in to the mobile app with their own account.

1. Create the person as a user first, with the Rider role. See "Invite a user".
2. Open "Riders" and tap "Add rider".
3. Choose the "Company" and the "User". Add their "Phone" and "Vehicle".
4. Tap "Save rider".

To stop using a rider, tap "Archive <name>". Their past deliveries stay on record.
