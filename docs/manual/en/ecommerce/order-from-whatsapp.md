Turn a customer's WhatsApp request into an order.

1. Open "WhatsApp inbox" and tap the conversation.
2. Tap "Create order".
3. Choose each "Product" and "Quantity". Tap "Add a product" for more.
4. Choose the "Delivery area", the "Payment", and type the "Customer name" and "Delivery address".
5. The total shows on the button. Tap "Create order for <amount>".

The order appears in "Online orders" and the conversation links to it by its number. The button is not offered once the chat is converted.
