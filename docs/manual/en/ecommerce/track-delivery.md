See where a rider is while an order is out for delivery.

1. Open "Deliveries" and tap "Track" on a card, or open the order and tap "Track".
2. "Delivery tracking" shows the "Last position", when it was "Reported", the route of the latest reports and the "Latest reports".
3. When the order is delivered, the "Proof photo" is available: tap "Open the proof photo".

The route is kept only while the delivery is under way and is cleared when it ends. If the rider has sent nothing yet, the page says "No position reported yet."
