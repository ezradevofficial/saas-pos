The reports show what the assets are worth and what changed.

1. Open "Reports" under "Fixed assets".
2. If you have several companies, choose one in the company switcher.
3. In "Net book value", read each category: the number of "Assets", the "Cost", the accumulated depreciation, the impairment and the net book value.
4. In "Depreciation", set "From" and "To", choose "Group by" "Category", "Branch" or "Cost centre", and read the charge of each group.
5. In "Additions and disposals", set "From" and "To" and read the assets that came in and the ones that left, with the gain or loss.
6. In "Tax book", choose the "Year" and read the "Value at the start", the "Allowance" and the "Value at the end". This tab shows only if you may see the tax book.

Costs and book values show only if you may see values. When there is nothing in the period, the tab says "Nothing to show."
