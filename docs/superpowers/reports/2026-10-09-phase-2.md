## Needed from the owner

   - KE `VAT_STD`: standard VAT rate (KRA / VAT Act).
   - KE `VAT_WHT`: withholding VAT rate, and who withholds it.
   - CD `VAT_STD`: standard TVA rate (DGI / Code de la TVA).

   - CD reduced TVA rates and point-of-sale withholding taxes, if any apply.
