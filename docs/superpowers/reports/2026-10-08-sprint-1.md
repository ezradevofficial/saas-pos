# Sprint 1 report: foundation

Date: 2026-10-08. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-07-sprint-1-foundation.md`.

## Outcome

All 15 tasks done, each reviewed (spec + quality) with fix rounds, followed by a whole-sprint review and one fix wave. CI on GitHub runs api tests, the isolation suite, web, pos, tokens and i18n jobs.

Exit criterion: a tenant signs up, signs in, creates companies, branches, locations, devices and users, and sees only what its role allows; the isolation suite proves tenant A cannot reach tenant B's rows through tables, routes (URL and body ids), lists or exports.
