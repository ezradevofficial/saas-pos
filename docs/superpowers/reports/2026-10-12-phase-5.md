# Phase 5 report: custom fields, templates, branding and layout designers

Date: 2026-10-12. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-phase-5-customisation.md`. ADRs: `010-versioned-configuration.md`, `011-custom-fields.md`, `012-custom-forms.md`, plus updates to `002` (public branding functions), `004` (new sync entities) and `005` (fonts). Server setup for branding: `docs/integrations.md`, section "Branding".

## Outcome

All seven Phase 5 tasks are on main, with their reviews fixed.

- **Versioned configuration** (LAY-06, LAY-07)
  - One store for themes, dashboards, menus, list views, form layouts, POS layouts and document templates.
  - Supports draft, publish, roll back, copy between places and history.
  - Resolution runs from user to role, location, branch, company and tenant.
  - Published versions can't change, and a stale draft edit is refused with a reload prompt.
  - Upgrade safety: new platform fields appear in a default place, and removed ones are skipped.
- **Custom fields** (CF-01..03, CF-06)
  - Eleven types on items, customers and suppliers.
  - Formulas are computed by our own evaluator, never `eval`.
  - Who may see or change each field is set per role.
  - Fields work in lists, filters, exports, workflow and automation conditions and template fields, and sync to the till when marked "show on the till".
- **Custom forms** (CF-04, CF-05)
  - Businesses build their own documents: fields, line tables with totals, attachments, numbering and an approval flow.
  - Each form gets its own menu entry, list, form and approvals.
- **Theme and branding** (BR-02..08)
  - A theme is a preset plus brand colours, sidebar, corners, curated font and logos, for the business, a company or a branch.
  - Contrast is checked on all four looks before publishing.
  - The sign-in page is branded on a subdomain or a verified custom domain.
  - Domains are verified by DNS, and certificates are issued on demand through Caddy.
  - Email can come from the business's verified domain, and an SMS sender ID is stored.
  - A per-business switch hides "Powered by"; plans will control it in phase 6.
- **Document templates** (TPL-01..05)
  - Block designer for 58/80 mm receipts and A4/A5 documents, in English, French or both.
  - Templates can vary by branch or customer tag.
  - The tax-authority block and tax lines are locked by country pack.
  - The server and the till render the same template from shared fixtures.
  - Output: print, PDF, email, and WhatsApp share through an expiring link.
- **Layout designers** (LAY-01..05)
  - Dashboards: 12-column grid with KPI, chart, list, shortcut and approval widgets; personal copies allowed.
  - Navigation per role, with a home page.
  - Saved list views, personal or shared.
  - Form layouts with sections, tabs and columns per role.
  - POS layout per location: grid, pinned items, category colours from tokens, quick buttons, sale panel side, customer display.
  - The till applies the theme and layout offline.

Exit criteria checked in the browser. Each task had its own check, and the closing walkthrough covered:

- a custom field on the item form;
- the Brand page with a published theme, and a refused low-contrast colour;
- domains with a pending TXT record;
- the receipt template with its tax block locked, and the receipt PDF and share link;
- the published Home dashboard and the dashboard designer;
- the menu editor for cashiers, saved list views and the form layout designer;
- a petty cash request created and approved from the inbox;
- the POS layout designer, the till following it, and the customer display.

## Reviews

Every risky task had its own review:

- the configuration core;
- custom fields;
- theme and branding;
- document templates.

The phase then had three closing reviews: custom forms; layouts and the POS layout; and the front end across both apps. All findings are fixed. Each fix has a test that fails without it.

The most important ones:

- **Custom forms:** a form limited to some roles was still visible, cancellable and returnable through the workflow screens. Hidden money fields leaked through totals and the inbox.
- **Branding:** one business could block another's custom domain for good.
- **Templates:** tax lines could be dropped from a Kenyan or Congolese receipt by removing the totals block.
- **Custom fields:** a formula could reveal a hidden field it reads, and archived role-limited fields showed in history.
- **Configuration:** with no revision check, publish could put another person's draft live. One malformed stored layout could break every lookup along its chain.
- **Designers:**
  - publishing after a failed save published the old draft;
  - Reload after a conflict did not reload;
  - a failed autosave showed "Saving…" for ever;
  - shared list views with a hidden-field filter broke the list for some readers.
- **Till:** images were fetched only when a screen drew them, so offline receipts printed without the logo.

The browser checks found two more:

- dialogs taller than the window could not be submitted;
- item forms jumped as their layout arrived.

Both are fixed.

## CI

Main went red six times in this phase. None were app bugs:

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Code style only, twice: two branches each clean, but the merge not | Pint now runs over the whole API before every push to main. |
| Test counts or lists that two branches each extended (permissions, notification events, the sync query budget) | Each merge now raises shared counts to the sum of both sides. |
| A long screen test hitting Jest's 5 s default | The screen tests now have 30 s. |
| Payment tests with a fixed date that aged past the 48-hour matching window | They now use the current time. |
| A web test that needed more than 1 s on a slow runner | Web tests now wait up to 4 s. |

Main is green at the end of the phase.

## Decisions made by the controller (say if you want otherwise)

- **Dependencies:** `@dnd-kit/core` and `@dnd-kit/sortable` (designers), `picqer/php-barcode-generator` (barcodes), and `@fontsource-variable/ibm-plex-sans` and `@fontsource-variable/newsreader` (the curated fonts, web only). The till maps those fonts to Geist.
- **Custom field rules:**
  - The Owner always sees and edits every field.
  - Writing a field you may not change is refused, not ignored.
  - A field shown on the till must be visible to everyone, because tills keep what they receive.
- **Custom forms:**
  - Generic `core.custom_form.*` permissions, plus a list of allowed roles per form.
  - Branch Manager and Accountant can view, create and approve.
  - Draft lines are updated in place and archived, never deleted.
  - A pending request must be cancelled before it can be archived.
- **Domains:**
  - The first business to verify a domain wins.
  - Verified domains are re-checked daily and dropped after three missing checks.
  - The TLS ask endpoint must be reachable only from Caddy.
- **Templates:**
  - Each "customer group" is a customer tag.
  - Tax-authority and totals blocks are locked wherever the country pack requires fiscal data.
  - Share links last 7 days.
- **Dashboards and the till:**
  - Dashboard numbers are cached for 45 seconds.
  - Each till's best-sellers are cached per location for 30 minutes.
  - The "keypad position" in the spec became the side of the sale panel, because the sell screen has no separate keypad.
  - Category colours use the five existing tints. There is no accent tint token; adding one is your call.
