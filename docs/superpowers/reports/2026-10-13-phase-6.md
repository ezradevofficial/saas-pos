## Outcome

All seven Phase 6 tasks are on main. Every review finding is fixed.

- **Platform staff and catalogue** (SUB-01)
  - Platform staff have their own accounts, sign-in with two-factor required, a console at `/platform` and their own audit chain. They have a separate database role and never touch tenant data directly.
  - The price list from concept note 8.4 is seeded as data: modules, units, prices in KES and USD, minimums, included quantities, bundles, add-ons, coupons and the annual discount.
  - Prices are effective-dated and never edited in place, so a change never touches existing subscriptions.
  - One integer-only price calculator serves the module picker, quotes and invoices. It suggests a bundle only when the bundle is cheaper.
- **Self-onboarding** (ONB-01..08)
  - Sign-up asks country and language first, then verifies email or phone by OTP. The subdomain is assigned at verification.
  - Eleven business types pre-select the POS mode, role templates, flows and suggested modules, all as data.
  - The module picker shows a live price and the bundle saving.
  - The trial is 14 days with no payment details, and a banner shows the days left.
  - The setup wizard has eight steps, each skippable and resumable.
  - Sample data loads and removes in one click. Removal is refused while the business's own records depend on it.
  - A getting-started checklist shows until the first sale.
  - Assisted onboarding: staff create a business, re-send the invitation, see businesses awaiting handover, and hand over ownership.
  - Abuse limits: per address, per IP and platform-wide. Protected brand names are refused, and unverified sign-ups are closed after 24 hours.
- **Subscriptions and billing** (SUB-02..07, LIM-05)
  - Upgrades apply at once and charge the remaining days. Downgrades and cycle changes wait for the period end.
  - The platform invoices as an internal operator business, with one selling company per country. Invoices therefore go through the same fiscal queue (eTIMS, DGI) and payment adapters as any customer's.
  - Payment methods:
    - M-Pesa;
    - Airtel Money, Orange Money and Afrimoney;
    - a hosted card checkout;
    - bank transfer marked paid by staff.

    Saved methods renew automatically. KES amounts are always whole shillings, so M-Pesa can collect them.
  - Dunning:
    - Reminders go out 7 and 3 days before the due date and on it.
    - After a grace period (7 days by default) the account becomes read-only.
    - Tills keep selling for 3 more days, then stop taking new sales. Sales already taken stay queued and upload once paid.
    - Paying reactivates at once.
  - Read-only never blocks approvals, account security actions or the data export.
  - A switched-off module keeps its data for 12 months. Staff get a report then; nothing is deleted.
  - Owners get a Subscription page (plan, live quote, invoices with PDF, payment, saved methods) and a status banner.
- **Usage limits** (LIM-01..05)
  - Outlets, devices, users and employees are counted daily (employees once HR exists).
  - When over the plan, the Owner gets an in-app and email warning with the extra cost and an Upgrade button, then a reminder each day for 7 days.
  - After day 7, only adding more of that item is blocked. Sales, approvals and the till are never blocked.
  - A till that was paired before can always be re-paired or resumed.
  - Overage goes on the next invoice at the add-on rate, pro-rated by days, and is charged once.
- **Import wizard** (IMP-01..03)
  - Imports items, customers, suppliers and prices.
  - Templates in en and fr.
  - Steps: upload xlsx or csv, map columns (mappings are remembered), preview with row errors, dry run, import.
  - Runs in the background with progress and a result file, and is tested to 50,000 rows.
  - The whole batch can be undone within 24 hours if nothing depends on it, in chunks, as the person undoing.
  - Custom fields go through the same validator as the forms.
  - Hostile files (wrong encoding, blank-row or zip bombs, huge cells) are refused.
- **Full data export** (EXP-02)
  - The Owner can request a machine-readable zip of every table and file, built under row-level security.
  - The download link expires after 5 minutes, works only for the requester, and needs the password confirmed.
  - Secrets are never included: password and PIN hashes, two-factor and device secrets, provider credentials, saved card tokens and phone numbers.

## Reviews

Every risky task had its own review: platform and catalogue, billing, limits, imports, export, and onboarding (because sign-up touches auth). A whole-phase review followed. Every finding is fixed, each with a test that fails without the fix.

The most important ones:

- **Billing:**
  - approvals were blocked in read-only, against the rule that approvals are never blocked;
  - sign-up never created a subscription, and onboarding and billing kept two different trial end dates;
  - paying after the due date flipped the account back to read-only within the hour;
  - some KES amounts had cents that M-Pesa cannot collect;
  - a payment could settle twice, and a second payment could be started while one was pending.
- **Limits:**
  - archiving below the limit, adding one, then restoring everything escaped the block;
  - billing never charged overage, and upgrading never lifted the block;
  - a till could not be re-paired after a reinstall while over the limit.
- **Onboarding:**
  - modules could be switched on after the trial ended;
  - Admins could change the subscription;
  - mass sign-ups could squat subdomains;
  - the setup account could sign in.
- **Imports:**
  - a job delivered twice could run twice;
  - undo could bypass field and scope rules;
  - other people could see an import's files;
  - hostile files could exhaust memory.
- **Export:** webhook URLs carried secrets, and the link lasted too long.
- **Whole phase:**
  - a read-only Owner could not download their export;
  - tills never actually stopped after the dunning window;
  - pre-Phase-6 businesses had no subscription (a backfill command now exists);
  - a few unlocked platform actions could race the billing run.

## CI

Main went red four times in this phase. None of them was a bug in the app:

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| A new queue (exports), a TOTP "wrong code" that was sometimes right, and Expo patch drift | The queue test reads each supervisor's own connection; the wrong code can never be valid; Expo is updated. |
| A secret-column check that needed each new column classified | The import columns are classified. |
| A test still expecting "import not available yet" after imports merged | The setup step links to the wizard for people who may import, and a test covers both cases. |
| Production-config tests unaware of billing drivers, and a click racing a re-render | The fixtures include billing drivers; the click targets the current button. |

The 50,000-row import test now runs as a separate `api (slow)` job. Agents now test against their own databases, because sharing one caused false failures.

## Decisions made by the controller (say if you want otherwise)

- **Prices:**
  - Prices include tax. Tax comes from the operator company's standard VAT code; until that rate is confirmed, platform invoices show no tax and the fiscal queue holds them for attention.
  - Pro-rated and overage amounts round to whole shillings.
  - A coupon applies to one period. A coupon added during a paid period applies from the next one.
- **Dunning and reactivation:**
  - Unpaid invoices are never voided automatically.
  - A read-only subscription is not renewed until it is paid.
  - Paying after months in read-only starts a fresh period from the payment date, with no back-charge for the read-only months.
  - When the POS window ends, Charge is disabled, but a sale already on the payment screen can be finished.
  - Read-only still allows approvals, security actions (sessions, sign-out everywhere, two-factor, own PIN) and the data export.
- **Who does what:**
  - Only the Owner may subscribe, change plans or modules, see usage, or export everything.
  - Admins get `core.onboarding.edit` for the setup steps, but not `core.onboarding.modules`.
- **Limits:**
  - Users are unlimited, because the catalogue sells no user unit.
  - A new 7-day window starts if usage goes back within the plan and then over again, unless that happens within 7 days of closing.
- **Onboarding:**
  - Platform staff are separate from tenant users.
  - Assisted businesses not handed over after 30 days are listed for staff, never deleted.
  - Unverified sign-ups are closed after 24 hours.
- **Imports:**
  - Only the uploader sees the preview, error and result files.
  - Undoing an item import can't reach tills that are offline and already hold the item. The undo dialog warns about this.
- **Dependencies:** none added on the web or the till. The API uses the existing spreadsheet reader.
