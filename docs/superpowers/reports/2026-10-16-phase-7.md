Date: 2026-10-16. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-13-phase-7-business-modules.md`.

Each module has a spec in `docs/modules/`, written before it was built: `inventory.md`, `loyalty.md`, `accounting.md`, `procurement.md`, `stores.md`, `hr.md`, `payroll.md`, `crm.md`, `fixed-assets.md`, `manufacturing.md` and `ecommerce.md`.

## Outcome

All eleven business modules are on main. Each was built in five steps:

1. Spec first.
2. Build, with its own security review.
3. Fixes for every finding, each with a test that fails without it.
4. A stage review and a browser walkthrough.
5. Fixes for those findings.

Every module works with only the core installed. Integrations switch on only while both modules are active (RBAC-08), and a module never reads another's tables. Modules talk only through events and narrow read contracts.

- Every business event posted exactly one balanced journal:
  - a till sale with a promotion and gift card, then a void and a refund;
  - a goods receipt, a landed cost and a supplier invoice;
  - a store issue and its return;
  - a payroll run and its correction;
  - an asset capitalisation, depreciation run and disposal;
  - a production order;
  - a restaurant sale with its recipe;
  - a CRM quote converted to an invoice.

- The ledger's stock account equalled the inventory valuation.

## Reviews

**Volume:**

- Every module had its own security review, and each stage had a combined review and a walkthrough.
- The reviews found roughly 30 high or critical defects and many medium ones.
- All are fixed, each with a test that fails without the fix.

**The most serious findings:**

- **E-commerce:**
  - Account takeover. A guest checkout could attach an attacker's phone to a customer's account, then sign in by SMS as them. The reviewer proved it with a test.
  - Guests could spend another customer's loyalty points.
  - Double payment and overselling.
- **Inventory:**
  - A void handled before its sale left stock out.
  - Stock moving during a count was counted twice.
- **Accounting:**
  - Back-dated postings could reopen closed years.
  - Any journal could be reversed, including invoices and the year-end close.
  - The consolidation elimination had the wrong sign.
  - Mixed-currency till payments never posted. This would have hit most DRC tenants.
- **Loyalty:**
  - Cancelled sales still earned points.
  - Unchecked "promotion" discounts bypassed cashier limits.
- **Procurement:**
  - Rotating the app key would have destroyed every sealed bid.
  - A tender could be awarded before all evaluators had scored.
  - The bidder list leaked before opening.
- **HR:**
  - Saving one ID number wiped the others.
  - Leave balances could go negative.
  - One branch could roster another branch's staff.
- **Payroll:**
  - Settled loans were still deducted.
  - Double corrections, and double pay across pay groups.
  - Overtime was never paid when attendance closed after the run.
  - A read-only billing state stopped tills from selling after a staff clock-in.
- **CRM:**
  - Hidden opportunity values leaked through totals.
  - Changing a quote's currency kept the old figures as typed prices.
- **Fixed assets:**
  - An account code collided with Payroll's staff loans account.
  - Capitalisation could happen twice.
  - Monthly runs raced with disposals.
- **Manufacturing:** a redelivered void was valued at zero.

## CI

CI now runs:

- the API tests in three shards;
- the tenant-isolation suite in five shards: exports, IDs, lists, tables and the rest;
- the slow group.

The test database's Postgres has 256 lock slots per transaction, because the isolation fixture touches every tenant table in one transaction.

Main went red several times in this phase. Every time, the cause was the build or the test suite, never an app bug:

- shards reaching the time limit;
- new tables, reasons or keys not yet listed in a shared check (export columns, import-undo reasons, print labels, permission counts);
- time-of-day-dependent tests;
- Expo patch drift, including a missing lockfile update;
- two flaky orderings.

Each was fixed, and each shared check now names exactly what is missing.

How CI is used changed during the phase: merges are grouped, and CI is checked at checkpoints rather than after every push.

## Decisions made by the controller or the owner (say if you want otherwise)

**Owner decisions:**

- **POS schema:** three optional generic fields on POS sales, lines and payments carry where a discount or tender came from. Loyalty uses them.
- **Landed costs:** Inventory accepts value-only cost adjustments, so late landed costs reach stock value.
- **Supplier portal:**
  - A read-only (overdue) tenant keeps the portal open; a suspended one closes it.
  - Bidders stay hidden until the opening.
  - The portal stays on the main origin, with a strict CSP.
- **HR:**
  - Self-service sign-ins don't count as users.
  - Payroll sold alone includes HR's employee core.
  - Rostered staff take leave only on rostered days.
- **CRM:**
  - Discounts over the limit go to approval.
  - A Sales Rep role exists.
- **Role limits:** editable per role copy, with separate till-discount, quote-discount, refund and approval limits.
- **Recipe costing:** full cost against the net price.

**Controller decisions, all recorded in the specs as changeable:**

- **Customer credit:** Loyalty's store credit posts to its own receivable account, and Accounting AR stays the invoice ledger.
- **Gift cards and points:**
  - Gift cards are a liability.
  - Points are a liability only when a point has a money value.
  - Promotions post to contra-revenue.
- **Posting:**
  - Accounting posts module events on Accounting being active alone, because an event shows the module was on when it happened.
  - A catch-up command posts history after Accounting is switched on.
- **Invoicing and approvals:**
  - Stock lines can't be invoiced before receipt.
  - Supplier invoices and payroll runs use maker-checker.
- **Payroll journals:**
  - Payroll posts at company level for departments under 3 people.
  - Approvers see run totals, not individual pay.
- **Attendance:**
  - An attendance period closes only 7 days after it ends, because tills can be offline that long.
  - Night shifts pair punches within 16 hours, which differs from HR-24's "previous punch of the day".
- **Default limits:**
  - Branch Manager: quote discount 10%, refund 10,000 in base currency.
  - Sales Rep: quote discount 0%.

## Owner inputs still needed

**Statutory and tax data.** None of it is invented. Each missing value blocks only the action that needs it.

- **HR:**
  - Statutory leave days and whether each type is paid. Kenya: annual, maternity, paternity, sick. DRC: annual, maternity, sick, circumstantial.
  - ID formats: KRA PIN, Kenyan national ID, DRC national ID, CNSS number.
- **Payroll, Kenya:**
  - PAYE bands and rounding.
  - Personal and insurance relief.
  - NSSF tiers.
  - SHIF.
  - Housing levy.
  - NITA.
  - The bases for each.
  - The weekly and bi-weekly rule.
  - Pension and mortgage relief, and the disability exemption.
  - Return and file layouts: P10, P9, NSSF, SHIF, housing levy and NITA returns, and the M-Pesa B2C file.
- **Payroll, DRC:**
  - IPR bands.
  - The dependant reduction.
  - CNSS pension, risk and family rates.
  - INPP by headcount.
  - ONEM.
  - Family allowance.
  - Return layouts.
- **Fixed assets:**
  - Kenyan wear-and-tear classes, rates, investment deduction, the year-of-acquisition rule, and the balancing allowance or charge.
  - DRC depreciation classes and rates.
  - Whether land qualifies, and how revaluation affects tax value.
- **Default accounts:** a DRC accountant should confirm the default accounts chosen for payroll, manufacturing and fixed assets (SYSCOHADA codes).

**Providers and production settings:**

- WhatsApp Business provider and credentials.
- A card payment provider per country.
- A maps provider for delivery tracking.
- Storefront CORS for tenant subdomains and custom domains.

**E-commerce decisions:**

**Earlier phases:** the Phase 4–6 inputs listed in their reports are still open: tax rates, Daraja, eTIMS, DGI, billing operator details, providers and branding DNS.
