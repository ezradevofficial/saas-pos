# Phase 4 report: offline sync and POS core

Date: 2026-10-09. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-phase-4-offline-sync-pos.md`. Module spec: `docs/modules/pos.md`. ADRs: `docs/adr/004-offline-sync.md` (now Accepted, with an Evidence section), `002-tenancy-rls.md`, `003-money-and-currency.md`. Integrations and what the owner provides: `docs/integrations.md`.

## Outcome

All eight Phase 4 tasks are on main.

- **POS module** (`api/modules/POS`, POS-01..13): sales, lines, payments, shifts and cash-up, cash movements, voids and refunds as new records, tax at sale, idempotent uploads by device UUID v7. The device wins for completed sales: differences are flagged for review, never refused. Unproven money going out (void, refund, pay-out without a verified approver) is held for back-office approval.
- **Numbering** (NUM-01, NUM-02): number formats with place and date tokens, per-device ranges allocated ahead and topped up below 100 left, next year's range handed over in the last 14 days of the year, and a history of every prefix a counter has used so a format or code change can never make two counters print the same number.
- **Sync** (NFR-04): per-entity pull with transaction-safe cursors and tombstones, snapshot entities, device bootstrap, rotating device secrets, POS PINs verifiable offline (AUTH-06), sign-in attestations (AUTH-07), manager overrides signed on the till and redeemed once (AUTH-08).
- **Payments**: adapters for cash, manual confirmation and M-Pesa Daraja (STK push, C2B, B2C refunds) with secured callbacks, lost-answer recovery, unmatched receipts, refund caps and rate limits.
- **Fiscal queue**: KRA eTIMS (OSCU) driver and a DGI e-MCF interface, retried until accepted, with back-office settings, a submission list, retries and "Send earlier sales".
- **POS app** (Expo, WatermelonDB): pairing, PIN sign-in and user switching, catalogue with search and barcode scan, cart, held sales, customers, split and multi-currency payment with change in another currency, M-Pesa push and typed codes, receipts with the fiscal state, printing, shifts and blind cash-up, voids and refunds with manager approval. Tablet and phone layouts.
- **Back office**: devices and pairing, POS PINs, receipt ranges, sales and shifts lists and detail, held review, consolidated sales dashboard across companies (TEN-07), payments received and matching, tax authority settings and queue.

Exit criteria checked end to end in the browser, POS through the Expo web preview against the real API:

3. The sale reached the server in about 250 ms, and the receipt shows the fiscal state.

## Reviews

Every risky task (POS module, sync and PINs, payments and fiscal, selling) had its own review and fix round; screens had browser checks.

The whole-phase review (three reviewers: core sync and auth, POS/payments/fiscal API, POS app and web) found, and these are fixed:

- **High: double refunds.** An M-Pesa refund whose request timed out was marked failed, so staff would refund again by hand while Safaricom may already have paid; its late result was then dropped. It is now `unknown`, checked until it gives up, and found by its own id.
- **High: lost or invalid payments at the till.** A cash payment added while an M-Pesa push was waiting could vanish when the push was paid, and an old copy of the payments could come back after a cart edit.
- **Paid by mobile money.** A sale already paid by M-Pesa could be edited, cleared or held, dropping the payment and asking the customer to pay again. Such a sale is now locked until completed.
- **Recorded twice.** A double tap could record a sale, refund or cash movement twice and burn a receipt number.
- **Fiscal documents that could never be sent.** These were lines without a tax code. The server now stores the code it resolved, and retries rebuild the document.
- **Flags added after a review stayed hidden.** They now re-open the review.
- **Price changes not checked.** A line sent without a list price skipped the check and the audit.
- **Receipt ranges arriving late.** New ranges reached the till only after a 30-second cache.
- **Duplicate receipt numbers.** Possible after a branch code swap or a format change back.
- **Lows:** loose override time format, PIN attempt counting races, flags written by two workers at once.

The browser walkthrough found, and these are fixed:

- A newly paired till never asked for receipt numbers.
- A USD till priced from the KES list.
- Cart line names cut to four letters.
- Enter did nothing in the payment amount field.
- A completed sale waited up to 30 s before uploading.
- Six review flags showed as raw codes.
- Refund and void flags were invisible in the back office: they now show on the sale and flag the sale for review.
- Tax authority labels used the code font.
- The devices API named its key-id field `secret`. No secret was ever sent, but the name was misleading and it failed a test.

## CI

Main went red three times in this phase, and each was fixed the same morning:

1. A devices test caught the misleading `secret` field.
2. The POS screen test failed after the upload-at-once change. I had merged that change after running only the sync and selling tests, and missed the screen test it affected. The first fix to the test was itself timing-dependent and was replaced.
3. The seven-day offline test failed after refund and void flags started flagging their sale. A resent sale now reports those flags, which is correct, so the test was updated and the whole POS module suite re-run.

In each case a change was merged without running every test it touched. The controller now runs the affected test folders and reads the test result before merging. Main is green at the end of the phase. Several runs were cancelled because a newer push replaced them.

## Decisions made by the controller (say if you want otherwise)

You asked for the phase to run without stopping, so every decision below was taken without asking.

- **Dependencies.** expo-camera and expo-print were added for scanning and printing.
- **Manager approvals at the till** are signed on the device and always flagged `override_offline` for review, never held. A server token expires in 2 minutes, while the record may upload much later.
- **Cash paid in** needs `pos.cash.move` or a manager, as pay-outs do.
- **Owners** have no till limits.
- **Shifts are never refused** for the opener's or closer's permission; they are stored and flagged.
- **Sales of a shift that never arrives** are stored after 72 hours on a placeholder shift, flagged `shift_missing` (`POS_UNKNOWN_SHIFT_GRACE_HOURS`).
- **Resetting another user's POS PIN** always asks for the admin's own password.
- **Sales and shift date filters** use each company's local days.
- **The sales overview** defaults to the first company's first reporting currency.
- **Until the till has a receipt number** it refuses to sell and says to go online.
- **A sale paid by M-Pesa** can't be edited, cleared, held or have that payment removed; a payment still pending can be removed (a late payment lands in "Payments received" for matching).
- **A flag on a refund or void** also flags its sale, so it appears in the sales review queue.
- **Non-KES sales in Kenya** are queued but held as "needs attention" until you decide how eTIMS should receive them.
- **Items are sent to eTIMS** with their own code and the company's default classification; registering items with KRA is a follow-up.

## Owner inputs still needed

- **Tax rates.** KE VAT standard and withholding, CD VAT standard ("Rate needed" blocks those items at the till). Movable public holidays for both countries.
- **M-Pesa Daraja.** Consumer key and secret, passkey, shortcode (paybill or till), initiator name and security credential for refunds, Safaricom's callback IP list, and the public callback URL (`docs/integrations.md`).
- **KRA eTIMS.** OSCU integrator approval, then per company: KRA PIN, branch id, device serial. The eTIMS band code for each tax code (A–E). KRA's duplicate-invoice result code (`ETIMS_DUPLICATE_CODES`). Whether OSCU needs invoice numbers in strict order (verify on the sandbox). How USD sales should be declared. The offline transmission deadline.
- **DGI e-MCF.** The API specification, credentials, tax groups, USD handling and the offline deadline. Until then DRC transmission can't be switched on.
- **Native Android check.** Build the development client on a real low-end Android phone to confirm WatermelonDB's JSI adapter under the New Architecture and the PIN check speed through the app's native PBKDF2 module.
- **Providers and server.** SMS and push providers; Linode secrets. On the server: PHP `pcntl` and `posix`, Horizon with the `payments` and `fiscal` queues, the scheduler, and `TRUSTED_PROXIES=192.168.255.0/24` behind the NodeBalancer.
- **Cashier discounts.** Should cashiers hold `pos.discount.give`, or should every discount need a manager?

## Follow-ups

- Refunds and voids have no review queue of their own; their flags reach review through the sale. A dedicated queue may be wanted once volumes grow.
- Register items with KRA (`saveItem`) and keep the KRA item codes.
- The local dev server (`php artisan serve`) handles one request at a time, so pages with many calls load slowly locally. Production runs PHP-FPM.
- Numbers issued before the prefix history existed are not protected against reuse (no backfill).
