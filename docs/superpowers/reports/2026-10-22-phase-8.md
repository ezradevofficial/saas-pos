## What was built, per requirement

### Public API (ADR 020)

- **API-01, the REST API.** `/api/v1` is the same API the web app uses.
  - Routes carrying `public-api:<module.resource>` accept keys. Every other route refuses them (403 `api_key_route_refused`), including sign-in, sessions, billing, platform, devices and key management.
  - Lists support `page`, `per_page`, `search`, a whitelisted `sort` and documented filters.
  - Errors are `{message, code}`. For keys they are served as `application/problem+json`.
  - A POST may send `Idempotency-Key`. The first answer is replayed for 24 hours, a different body gets 422, and a request still running gets 409.
- **API-02, keys.**
  - A key is a service principal: a `users` row with status `service`. It holds one role assignment, granted by the same anti-escalation rule as for people, never an Owner role. Scopes (`module.resource.read|write`) narrow it twice: per route, and per permission check inside `ScopeResolver`.
  - The secret (`sk_live_…`, or `sk_test_…` in a sandbox) is shown once. Only its sha256 is stored.
  - Rotation keeps the old secret working for 0 to 168 hours.
  - Every create, update, rotate and revoke is audited.
- **API-03, rate limits.**
  - Fixed one-minute windows per key and per tenant. The tenant limit comes from the plan.
  - Responses carry `RateLimit-*` headers. A refusal is 429 with `Retry-After`.
  - Failed key authentications are limited per IP.
  - Usage is counted in Redis and flushed to `api_key_usage_days` every 5 minutes.
- **API-04, documentation.**
  - Scramble generates OpenAPI 3.1, and the document is committed (`resources/openapi/v1.json`).
  - The `/developers` portal (en/fr) has guides, error codes, a changelog and a reference rendered from that document.
  - In CI, `api:docs --check` fails when an operation is removed without a deprecation entry, checked against the base branch's document.
  - Deprecations send `Deprecation`, `Sunset` and `Link` headers.
- **API-05, the sandbox.**
  - Self-serve sign-up with every module on and sample data loaded.
  - Hard caps: 3 users, 2 devices, 2 outlets, 500 records and 1,000 requests a day.
  - It closes after 30 days idle.
  - Fake payment and fiscal drivers. Every document is stamped "SANDBOX – not a fiscal receipt". Messages go to an outbox shown on Settings, API keys.
  - The Owner can reset it. It is never billed.

### Webhooks (ADR 021, `docs/webhooks.md`)

- **HOOK-01, subscriptions.**
  - Subscriptions to a versioned public catalogue:
    - `sale.completed/voided/refunded.v1`
    - `stock.low.v1`
    - `invoice.posted.v1` and `credit_note.posted.v1`
    - `supplier_invoice.approved.v1` and `purchase_order.approved.v1`
    - `payroll.approved.v1`
    - `order.*.v1`
    - `document.stage_changed.v1`
    - `webhook.test.v1`
  - Each module registers its own types. They are offered and sent only while the module is active (RBAC-08).
- **Payloads.**
  - Payloads carry ids and minimal fields, filtered by the subscribing principal's permissions at the event's place and by its field rules. Hidden values are never stored or sent.
  - Signing follows Standard Webhooks (HMAC-SHA256 over id, timestamp and body). The secret is shown once, and rotation has a 24-hour dual signature.
- **Delivery.**
  - Deliveries run on their own queue and Redis connection.
  - Retries at 1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 2 h, 6 h and 24 h, then a dead-letter `failed` state. A subscription switches itself off after 10 consecutive failures, and the webhook managers are notified.
  - The delivery log keeps 30 days and supports replay.
  - The URL is HTTPS only, SSRF-guarded on save and on send, pinned to the checked address, and redirects are never followed.

### Super-admin console (`docs/modules/platform-admin.md`, ADR 022)

- **ADM-01, tenants.** Search by name, owner email (audited), country, plan, status and module. The detail page shows plan, modules, usage, health and invoices. Staff can suspend, reactivate, extend a trial and grant a grace period, each with a reason. A suspended tenant keeps billing, export, approvals and payroll (owner decision 2026-10-17).
- **ADM-02, Enterprise deals.** Sales or finance drafts a deal, and a different finance member approves it. Quotes and renewals use the deal.
- **ADM-03 and RBAC-13, support access with consent.**
  - Staff ask with a reason, a mode and a number of hours. The tenant's Owners and Admins approve, narrow (read-only, sensitive data off by default), deny or revoke.
  - The session is a support principal with no role. It never holds credential-making permissions: API keys, webhooks, device pairing, automation, shares and portal links.
  - Writes are logged before they run (fail closed). Every request is in both audit chains and visible to the tenant.
  - The entry code is single-use, lasts 60 seconds and is bound to the console browser.
- **ADM-04, country pack releases.**
  - Load, then validate and diff, then approve (not by the loader), then publish (not by the approver). Each release has an effective date.
  - Releases are rows that override the shipped files, and every pack reader uses them.
  - Tax and holiday releases are applied on their date, with one job per tenant, suspended tenants included.
- **ADM-06, reports.** MRR (net of tax), trials, conversion, churn, revenue by country, module and plan, and usage, each per currency and as CSV.
- **ADM-07, staff roles.** Six fixed roles: owner, support, sales, finance, compliance and engineering. A permission-matrix test covers every console route.

### Marketplace (ADR 024, `docs/webhooks.md`)

- **MKT-01, developers and apps.**
  - A developer account belongs to a tenant: a sandbox or a live one. It registers once with a public name, a contact email and the terms (80/20).
  - An app has a slug, one OAuth client and a webhook signing secret. Both secrets are shown once, can be rotated, and are kept where no runtime role can read them.
  - What a tenant consents to is a version: texts, logo, redirect URIs, scopes, webhook URL and events, and price. Versions are drafted, submitted, then approved or rejected. A reviewed version never changes.
- **ADM-05, review.**
  - Staff see what a version adds to the listed one: scopes, events, redirect URIs, price and consent.
  - Approving lists the version. If the version changed after the reviewer opened it, approval is refused with 409 `version_changed`.
  - Rejecting needs a reason. Suspending stops tokens, webhooks and installs at once.
  - Every decision is audited in the platform chain and the developer's.
- **MKT-02 and API-02, install with consent.**
  - The web app's `/oauth/authorize` shows, in plain words, what the app will be able to do and its price. The installer picks a role and a place, by the same anti-escalation rule as API keys, never an Owner role.
  - Approving creates a service principal with a key of kind `app`, one role assignment, the install, and its webhook subscription signed with the install's own key.
  - Passport serves only the token endpoint: authorization code with S256 PKCE, and rotating refresh tokens with reuse detection.
  - Tokens are RS256 JWTs. They are stored hashed and live in the install's tenant under RLS.
  - Uninstalling revokes every code and token, the key, the role assignment and the subscription. The data the app created stays.
- **MKT-03, paid apps.**
  - Prices are monthly per currency. Each period invoice carries one `app` line per paid, active install. During a paid period, the rest of the period is invoiced at once, pro rata. During the trial nothing is charged.
  - When the invoice is paid, the developer earns 80% of the line, net of discount and tax, in an append-only ledger in the operator tenant.
  - Payouts are recorded by hand: never more than the balance, and audited.
- **MKT-04, first-party connectors.**
  - Four free apps of the platform's own developer: Courier, SMS and WhatsApp, Power BI and Excel (CSV feeds of the list endpoints), and Biometric attendance.
  - Each stays "not set up yet" until the owner configures its service with `marketplace:connector`.
  - `GET marketplace/connectors` names what each still lacks.
- **The role pickers ask the API.** `GET roles?grantable_at=…` returns what `Grants` allows, so an Owner in a tenant without every module can pick Admin. This closes the earlier role-picker finding.

### Enterprise SSO (ADR 023)

- **AUTH-11.** Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace by OIDC, and any IdP by SAML 2.0, configured per tenant.
- **Email domains.** Each domain is proven by a DNS TXT record. The first verifier wins, and domains are re-checked daily.
- **Finding the tenant.** By email domain on the shared sign-in page, or by the tenant's own host.
- **OIDC checks.** PKCE, nonce and state, an ID token checked against the IdP's JWKS (RS256), and a pinned Entra directory (`common` is refused).
- **SAML checks.** SP-initiated only, a signed response or assertion, replay protection, and transient NameIDs refused.
- **Linking.** In order: an existing link, then a verified user with the same email, then an open invitation, then just-in-time provisioning (off by default). An Owner is never linked by email and links their own account from Settings, Security.
- **Enforced SSO.** Turns password sign-in off for the verified domains. Owners keep a break-glass password and two-factor.
- **Second factor.** The IdP's MFA assertion stands in for the app's.
- **Testing a connection.** A connection must pass a test before it can be enabled. Repointing it archives its links and disables it.

### Researched country pack values (`docs/country-packs-research-2026-10.md`)

- **What was applied.** Values of high or medium confidence went in as pack rows with their status, confidence, sources and research date:

  - CD: VAT, IPR bands with the 30% cap and the minimum, CNSS, ONEM, maternity leave and circumstantial leave.
- **Where it shows.** Runs, tax lines, leave records, the tax-codes export and the till's synced rates say when a value is researched. The payroll statutory rates page shows the researched notice and every assumption.
- **What was left out.** Values of low confidence stayed null and kept blocking what needs them, until the owner decision below.
- **Applied 2026-10-22 by owner decision** (knowing they are not confirmed by an accountant; branch `feat/researched-pack-values-2`). Every value carries its sources, `researched_on` and confidence; the list is in the research doc, "Applied 2026-10-22 by owner decision":

  - Low-confidence values show "Researched, low confidence: not confirmed by an accountant" on runs, tax codes, leave types and requests, the statutory rates page and the tax classes, and the pack release validator warns on each.

## Security reviews and fixes

Every Phase 8 task had its own security review. Every finding is fixed, each with a test that fails without it. The most serious, by area:

- **Public API.**
  - Rotating or rescoping a key did not re-check the right to grant its role, so a key manager could take over a key they could not have made. It now needs `Grants::assertCanGrant`, and revocation goes through `RoleManager`.
  - The sandbox had no abuse controls. It now has sign-up limits per IP, domain and overall, hard caps, 30-day expiry, the document stamp and the outbox, and is set up only after verification.
  - The idempotency claim could be stuck for a day by a dead worker. It now has a 60-second lock and takeover, the hash covers the full URI, and expired rows are pruned on schedule.
  - Usage was written per request. It is now counted in the cache after admission.
  - Failed key attempts are now limited per IP.
  - The OpenAPI removal check could be bypassed by rewriting the baseline. CI now checks against the base branch's document.
  - Principals could be taken for people (approvers, owners, reviewers and others). `User::isPerson()` and active-only lookups now prevent that.
- **Webhooks (ADR 021, findings 1 to 10).**
  - Anyone with `core.webhook.manage` could re-point another principal's subscription. Only the owner of a subscription changes its URL or events, and an Owner may take it over (audited).
  - Permissions and field rules were checked only at fan-out. They are now re-checked at send, replay and log view.
  - A URL path could carry a token. Paths are masked everywhere.
  - Two concurrent creates could pass the subscription cap. The cap now counts under an advisory lock.
  - A fan-out run twice could write duplicate deliveries. Each event now gets exactly one delivery row.
  - Queue payloads carried more than needed, and `occurred_at` was not always the event's own time. Both are fixed.
- **Super-admin and support access.**
  - Support sessions could leave credentials behind (H1, H2): device pairing, automation rules, shares, portal links and API keys. Those permissions are now never granted to a session. Ending a grant suspends the devices it touched and switches off the rules it changed.
  - Changing a staff member's role or deactivating them did not end their access. It now ends their grants in every tenant.
  - Session logging failed open (M8). Writes are now refused when their audit entry cannot be written.
  - Support principals got only the default field rules (M6). They now get the most restrictive rules.
  - Ended deals stopped blocking overlapping deals (M7). They now block them until their last day.
  - Tax releases skipped suspended tenants (H3, M4). Propagation now runs as one job per tenant, and the runtime role reads published releases only.
  - Low items L1 to L10:
    - The entry code is bound to the console browser.
    - Signed links made in a session end with the grant.
    - Owner-email search is audited.
    - MRR by module adds up to MRR.
  - A support session could manage webhooks. It no longer can.
  - MRR and churned MRR were gross of tax while revenue was net. Both are now net of tax, in the reports and the tenants list.
- **SSO.**
  - Entra links trusted any `email` claim. They now use the UPN unless `xms_edov` is true.
  - An Owner could be linked by email, which lets whoever controls the IdP take over the Owner. Owners now link only themselves.
  - Google trusted any `hd` claim. Only verified domains are trusted now.
  - Repointing a connection to another IdP kept its links. It now archives them and disables the connection until a new test passes.
  - A transient SAML NameID was accepted. It is now refused.
  - The SSRF guard on metadata URLs missed carrier-grade NAT, NAT64 and 6to4 ranges. They are now refused, and the body is streamed with a byte cap.
  - A rotated SAML certificate broke sign-in. A daily refresh now adds it.
  - Invitees of an enforced domain could join with a password. They now join with SSO.
- **Marketplace.**
  - **High 1:** app-owned webhook subscriptions could be taken over or re-pointed by people. People may now only switch them off. Each install signs with its own derived key, and the signed body names the install and the tenant.
  - **Medium 2:** app secrets could be read by tenant sessions. They moved to a table with no runtime grant, reached only through security-definer functions that never return a secret.
  - **Medium 3:** a version changed after the reviewer opened it could be approved. Approval now locks the version and compares its submission time (409 `version_changed`).
  - **Medium 4:** insert policies didn't tie rows to the session's developer, and a developer could change its client's redirect URIs or revoked flag. Both are fixed.
  - **Medium 5:** the role pickers could disagree with `Grants`. They now ask the API.
  - **Low 6:**
    - Consenting again needs the right to revoke the old role.
    - Code and refresh-token redemption are atomic.
    - A suspended developer stops its apps.
    - A leaked webhook secret can be ended at once.
  - **Scrubber:** OAuth code verifiers, client secrets and refresh tokens are now scrubbed from logs.
- **Hardening and privacy.**
  - Access reports followed foreign keys (H1), so a cashier's sales appeared in their own report. They now read only the tables each module declares.
  - HR's sensitive groups were not masked in access reports (H2). They are now, and showing them is audited.
  - Fiscal customers were fully anonymised (M3). They now keep their tax ID and legal name for refunds and voids sent to KRA eTIMS or the DGI.
  - User deletion skipped user-management rules (M4, M5). It now follows them.
  - `/metrics` could be reached through the load balancer (M1). It no longer can, readiness is cached, and method labels are bounded.
  - The scrubber missed several secrets (M2): one-time codes, token-like path segments, long integers and SQL bindings. All are scrubbed now.
  - npm audit exceptions now need a review within 90 days.
- **Researched packs.**
  - Value rows now always state their status.
  - Assumptions are shown on the rates page and on runs.
  - Not-applicable marks are tied to their period and have their own permission.
  - Half-pay sick days are counted over a rolling 12 months.
  - The KE national ID format only warns, because it has no authoritative source.
  - A tenant can replace a researched rate from its own start date.

## Whole-phase review

After the stages merged, the whole phase was reviewed again as one system: 2 high, 7 medium and 11 low findings. The fixes came on two branches, `fix/p8-review-support-sso-privacy` and `fix/p8-review-sandbox-limits-scrub`, both merged. Each fix has a test that fails without it.

2. **No standalone `http2` directive in nginx 1.24 (`f1f5dd7f`).** That directive arrived in nginx 1.25.1. The HTTPS template now sets `http2` on the `listen` line.
3. **Deploy secrets that replaced `PATH` (`a3d4c760`).** The `workflow_call` secrets named `host`, `user` and `path` replaced `HOST`, `USER` and `PATH` in the deploy job. `PATH` became `/srv/platform`, so npm reported "spawn sh ENOENT". The secrets are renamed `deploy-host`, `deploy-user` and `deploy-path`. A first, wrong guess (installing without scripts) and a diagnostic step (`4f269b9c`, `12772745`) came before it; the rename undid both.
4. **CORS without the console host (`eebbc895`).** `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` listed only the app host, so the console's setup page could not reach the API. Fixed in the server's `.env`, and the template in `docs/deploy-linode.md` lists both.
5. **CI's shellcheck SC2015 findings (`4f269b9c`).** Three `A && B || die` guards in the host scripts became `if` statements.
6. **A time-dependent CSV assertion (`4f269b9c`).** `CustomFieldValuesTest` looked for the hidden value `42` anywhere in the CSV, and a Created time of 17:42 matched. It now looks for a whole cell.

**Product.** Four changes to the decisions listed below, built on `feat/owner-decisions-2026-10-22`:

1. **STOP covers all channels.** A STOP reply by SMS or WhatsApp withdraws marketing on every channel (email, SMS and WhatsApp), not only the one it came by (NFR-08; `MarketingOptOutTest`, E-commerce `MarketingConsentTest`).
2. **One consent box.** The storefront keeps its single box for SMS, WhatsApp and email together (wording version `2026_10`); no box per channel.
3. **The buyer snapshot.** Each POS sale stores the buyer's KRA PIN or NIF and name (`pos_sales.buyer_tax_id`, `buyer_name`) when its customer has a tax ID, from the till (sync payload and the till's database, schema v10) or else from the customer at upload; existing sales were backfilled in chunks. Fiscal documents, refunds, voids and reprints read the snapshot, never the live party, so a customer with fiscal sales is now fully anonymised once none of their fiscal submissions is pending (`fiscal_pending` stays). This replaces keeping the tax ID and legal name on the party (POS-08, POS-10, NFR-08; `BuyerSnapshotTest`, `PrivacyFiscalTest`).
4. **Coupons.** A plan coupon (and the annual discount) applies to plan lines only and never lowers the developer's 80 % (MKT-03, SUB-03; ADR 024 decision 9; `PaidAppsTest`).

**Infrastructure:**

- Paystack was considered; Daraja stays the payment provider for now.
- Namecheap DNS is chosen over Cloudflare. ADR 025's note about keeping Cloudflare records "DNS only" applies to any proxying DNS provider; Namecheap records do not proxy.
- Single-host staging and pilot with self-managed PostgreSQL (ADR 025).
- **Staging and pilot setup:** one Linode, Object Storage for files, email only (no SMS provider yet), the Daraja sandbox, no backups yet and no Sentry for staging (ADR 025, `docs/deploy-linode.md`: PostgreSQL runs on that host).

**Public API (ADR 020):**

- A key holds exactly one role at one place, never an Owner role. A key's writes are attributed to its service user. *Accepted by the owner 2026-10-22.*

- A token's last use is recorded at most once a minute, so the idle timeout may end a session up to 60 seconds early, never late (AUTH-09). *Accepted by the owner 2026-10-22.*
- One API version in the path. A removal needs a deprecation with a sunset at least 6 months later. *Accepted by the owner 2026-10-22.*
